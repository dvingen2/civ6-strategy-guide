# Foundations: Core Concepts & Terminology

A primer for players new to Civ 6. Skip to any section you need.

---

## The Goal: Victory Conditions

There are six ways to win. You pursue one (or hedge across a few):

| Victory Type | How You Win | Arabia Suitability |
|-------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Domination** | Capture every other civ's original capital | Viable (Mamluk helps) |
| **Science** | Build and launch a Space Race project | Strong (Madrasa synergy) |
| **Culture** | Attract more visiting tourists than any civ has domestic tourists | Viable |
| **Religion** | Convert 51%+ of all other civs' cities to your religion | **Primary strength** |
| **Diplomatic** | Earn Diplomatic Victory Points via World Congress | Situational |
| **Score** | Highest score when turn limit ends | Fallback |

> **Arabia's sweet spot:** Religion first, Science second. The two reinforce each other.

## Yields: The Six Resources

Everything you build, research, or do costs or produces one or more yields:

| Yield | Icon | What It Does |
|-------|------|-------------|
| **Production** | ⚙️ | Builds units, districts, buildings, wonders |
| **Food** | 🌾 | Grows your city population |
| **Gold** | 💰 | Buys units/buildings, pays maintenance |
| **Science** | 🔬 | Unlocks technologies |
| **Culture** | 🎭 | Unlocks civics (social policies) |
| **Faith** | ✝️ | Founds religions, buys religious units and buildings |

> **Key insight:** You almost never have enough of everything. Arabia specialises in Faith, Science, and Gold — lean into that.

## Cities: How They Work

Each city has:
- **City Center** — the founding tile; always your first "district"
- **Population** — grows with Food; more pop = more tiles worked = more yields
- **Housing** — caps population growth (from buildings, water, improvements)
- **Amenities** — happiness; too few = growth/production penalties
- **Districts** — specialised zones you build (see below)

### District Slots
Cities unlock district slots based on population thresholds (roughly every 3–4 pop). Districts are expensive — prioritise ruthlessly.

## Districts: The Core of City Development

Districts are the main investment in any city. Each one unlocks buildings.

| District | Primary Yield | Key Buildings |
|---------|--------------|--------------|
| **Holy Site** | Faith | Shrine, Temple, Worship Building |
| **Campus** | Science | Library, University (→ Madrasa for Arabia) |
| **Commercial Hub** | Gold + Trade Routes | Market, Bank |
| **Harbor** | Gold + Trade Routes | Lighthouse, Shipyard |
| **Theater Square** | Culture | Amphitheater, Art Museum |
| **Encampment** | Military | Barracks, Armory |
| **Industrial Zone** | Production | Workshop, Factory |
| **Entertainment Complex** | Amenities | Arena, Zoo |

**Arabia priority:** Holy Site first (almost always), Campus second.

### District Adjacency Bonuses
Districts earn bonus yields based on what surrounds them:
- Holy Site: +1 Faith per adjacent Mountain; +2 Faith per adjacent Natural Wonder; +0.5 per 2 adjacent Forest/Woods; Rainforest only with Sacred Path pantheon
- Campus: +1 Science per adjacent Mountain; +0.5 per 2 adjacent Rainforest; +2 per Geothermal Fissure

> **Arabia key mechanic:** The Madrasa (Arabia's unique Campus building) gives +5 Science base, plus **Faith equal to the Campus district's adjacency bonus**. A Campus with +4 adjacency (from 4 adjacent Mountains) = +4 Faith from the Madrasa. Mountains benefit both districts simultaneously — place Holy Site and Campus near the same mountains for maximum combined yield.

## Technologies vs. Civics

Two separate research trees:

- **Technology Tree** — researched with Science. Unlocks units, buildings, improvements.
- **Civics Tree** — researched with Culture. Unlocks governments, policies, wonders.

Both run simultaneously. You're always researching one tech and one civic at a time.

### Boosts (Eureka/Inspiration)
Most techs and civics have a **boost condition** — a specific action that cuts the research cost in half. Always try to trigger these before starting research.

Example: The "Writing" tech boost is triggered by building two districts. Try to build a second district *before* queuing Writing.

## Great People

Specialists that provide powerful one-time or ongoing bonuses:

| Type | How to Earn | Relevance for Arabia |
|------|------------|---------------------|
| **Great Prophet** | Faith output over time | **Critical** — founds your religion |
| **Great Scientist** | Science output | High value |
| **Great Merchant** | Gold/trade | Useful |
| **Great Admiral/General** | Military | Situational |
| **Great Artist/Writer/Musician** | Culture/Tourism | Late game |

> **Arabia's passive safety net:** If Arabia hasn't founded a religion when the last Great Prophet spawns, they automatically receive it. You will always get a religion — but acting early gives you the best Pantheon and religious belief choices.

## Religion: How It Works

1. **Pantheon** — Chosen early with just 25 Faith. Provides a small, permanent bonus. Arabia wants a Pantheon that boosts Faith generation or helps Holy Site adjacency.
2. **Great Prophet** — Earns enough Faith (or is attracted by high Faith output) to spawn. Use it to **Found a Religion** and choose two Beliefs.
3. **Beliefs** — You choose one Founder Belief and one Follower Belief (later a Worship Building and an Enhancer). These shape what bonuses your religion provides.
4. **Spreading** — Use Missionaries and Apostles to spread your religion to other cities. Theological Combat (Apostle vs Apostle) can defend or attack.
5. **Religious Victory** — Your religion must be the majority religion in 51%+ of all other civs' cities when the end-of-turn check fires.

### Worship Buildings (Arabia-specific)
Arabia's unique mechanic: whichever Worship Building type your religion uses (e.g. Cathedral, Mosque, Pagoda, Stupa) costs only 10% of its normal Production cost. This makes it fast to build across your empire, amplifying whatever bonus that building provides.

## Governments & Policy Cards

As you research Civics, you unlock different governments:

| Government | Era | Character |
|-----------|-----|----------|
| Chiefdom | Ancient | Default |
| Autocracy | Ancient | Military slant |
| Classical Republic | Classical | Culture/production |
| Oligarchy | Classical | Military |
| Merchant Republic | Medieval | Trade/gold |
| Theocracy | Medieval | **Arabia favourite** — Faith bonuses |
| Monarchy | Medieval | Military/housing |
| Democracy | Industrial | Science/Culture |
| Fascism | Modern | Military |
| Communism | Modern | Production |
| Corporate Libertarianism | Information | Gold/Science |
| Digital Democracy | Information | Science/Culture |

Each government has **policy card slots** (Military, Economic, Diplomatic, Wildcard). You fill these with unlocked policies for passive bonuses.

> **Key habit:** Review your policies when you change governments or unlock new ones. Good policies can double your yields.

## Eras & Ages

The game progresses through eight eras:
1. Ancient
2. Classical
3. Medieval
4. Renaissance
5. Industrial
6. Modern
7. Atomic
8. Information

Each era has a **Historic Moment** score system. Accumulating enough moments before the era ends determines your next **Age** (Dark, Normal, Heroic, Golden). Golden Ages give powerful bonuses; Dark Ages give penalties but unlock powerful Legacy Bonuses in the *next* era.

## Trade Routes

Trade Routes are a major yield source, established by Traders (built at Commercial Hub or Harbor):
- **Domestic route**: Sends Food and Production to a city you choose
- **International route**: Sends Gold to your city, Science/Culture to the target civ

> Arabia benefits enormously from international trade routes — they generate gold income and passive diplomatic goodwill.

---

## Espionage (Mid-Game Onward)

Unlocked with the Castles civic. Spies can:
- Steal technologies from foreign cities
- Siphon gold
- Sabotage districts
- Counter enemy spies

Not a primary focus for Arabia, but don't ignore it entirely.

## Combat Basics

- **Melee units** can capture cities; ranged units cannot
- **Zone of Control**: Adjacent melee units limit enemy movement
- **Flanking**: Attack an enemy unit from two sides for a damage bonus
- **Support units** (Battering Rams, Siege Towers) help assault city walls
- **Pillaging**: Damaged units can pillage improvements for a small heal/yield

> Arabia's Mamluk heals every turn regardless of actions — this fundamentally changes offensive warfare. You can push further and harder with Mamluks than any other Medieval cavalry.

## Map & Terrain Basics

| Feature | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Hills** | +1 Production; units defend better; slow movement |
| **Mountains** | Impassable (without Mountain Warfare policy); +Faith/Science adjacency for districts |
| **Rainforest** | +1 adjacency for Campus/Holy Site; provides Appeal |
| **Desert** | Low yields normally; Arabia's Madrasa + Holy Site synergy partially offsets this |
| **Oasis** | +3 Food, +1 Production, +1 Gold on Desert; very valuable for Arabia |
| **Rivers** | +1 Housing in a city; adjacency for Commercial Hub/Aqueduct |

**Arabia has a Desert start bias** — you'll frequently start near Desert tiles. This makes Mountains more valuable for district placement, and Oasis tiles extremely precious.

## Key Habits for Beginners

1. **Always be researching** — never let your Science or Culture queue run empty
2. **Always be building** — production queue in every city, always
3. **Scout early** — find natural wonders, city-states, and map features ASAP
4. **Settle city 2 fast** — second city around T15–25 is usually optimal
5. **Boost before researching** — trigger Eureka/Inspiration conditions first
6. **Watch Amenities** — too few and your cities stop growing and producing efficiently
7. **Trade routes are free yield** — send Traders immediately when unlocked
8. **Religion is time-sensitive** — only 7 Great Prophets exist; claim one early
