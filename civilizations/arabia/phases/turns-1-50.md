# Turn-by-Turn Script: Turns 1–50

*Saladin Vizier, Standard speed, Prince difficulty. The most prescriptive guidance in the framework.*

*[← Overview](../overview.md) | [Early Game →](early-game.md)*

---

## Before Turn 1: Starting Position Assessment (2 minutes)

Before pressing anything, spend 60 seconds reading your map.

**Scan checklist:**
- [ ] Mountains within 3 hexes of your Settler — these power both Campus and Holy Site adjacency later
- [ ] River nearby? (Housing + Commercial Hub eligibility)
- [ ] Oasis tiles? (Food + Production + Gold — very valuable in desert starts)
- [ ] Desert tiles? (Desert Folklore gives +1 Faith each)
- [ ] Natural Wonder visible? (Move Scout toward it — triggers Astrology Eureka)
- [ ] Starting tile Production potential?

**Move or settle decision:**
```
River nearby AND Mountains within 3 hexes → SETTLE NOW
One better tile visible 1–2 steps away → MOVE THEN SETTLE
Uncertain → SETTLE, don't overthink it
```

> Moving your Settler one tile costs nothing. Moving three tiles for uncertain improvement usually isn't worth it.

---

## Turn 1

**Actions:**
1. Settle your capital
2. Queue **Scout** as first build
3. Move **Warrior** in any unscouted direction

**Research:** Start **Pottery** (unlocks Shrine — your first Faith building).
**Civics:** Start **Code of Laws** (first civic; unlocks early policy slots).

**Why Scout first:** Finding a Natural Wonder triggers the Astrology Eureka, which halves its cost. This matters because you will build Astrology eventually — and you want to build it when the Eureka fires, not chase it early. The Scout also reveals Barbarian camps before they threaten your cities.

---

## Turns 1–5: Early Scouting Phase

**Scout:** Move 3–4 tiles per turn using open terrain. Cover as much ground as possible.
**Warrior:** Patrol near your capital. Don't wander — keep it available for Barbarian defense.

**Watch for:**
- Natural Wonder tiles → note location; move Scout toward them
- Barbarian Scout units (if one spots your city, a war party spawns — kill it before it reaches camp)
- City-states → meet them for first Envoy placement
- Good second city locations (Mountains + River)

---

## Turn 5–8: First Production Decision

Scout should be nearly done.

**Queue next build — decision:**
```
Barbarian Scout spotted near city?
  YES → Build Warrior (protect your city first)
  NO  → Build Monument
```

**Default: Monument.** +2 Culture/turn accelerates your civic tree — faster Craftsmanship, faster Bronze Age, faster Theocracy later. Every turn of Culture compounds.

---

## Turn 8–15: Planning Your Second City

When Pottery finishes, you can build a Shrine. **Don't queue it yet in the capital** — queue a **Settler** instead if you haven't started one.

**Research after Pottery:**
- **Animal Husbandry** — reveals Horse resources. You must know where Horses are before Medieval (Mamluks need them). This is not optional.
- After Animal Husbandry: **Mining** or **Sailing** depending on start, then **Astrology** when a Natural Wonder is found.

**Why defer the Shrine:** A Shrine gives ~1–2 Faith/turn. A second city gives you another city's worth of everything. At this stage, settlement expansion outweighs Faith accumulation. You'll build Shrines in every city — just not at the expense of getting that city settled first.

**Exception:** If you have 5+ Desert tiles and have chosen (or will choose) Desert Folklore, a Shrine in your capital starts generating significant Faith immediately. In this case: Shrine → Settler, not Settler → Shrine.

---

## Turn 12–20: Second City

Your second city is the most important action in this window. Aim to settle it by **Turn 18–22**.

**Settler siting checklist:**
1. Mountains within 3 hexes of a Campus spot — this is Arabia's core engine
2. River access for Housing
3. Horses visible nearby or in the second city's territory
4. Minimum 4 tiles from capital (avoid tile overlap), ideally 5–6
5. No active Barbarian camp adjacent

**When the Settler arrives and settles:**
```
Queue: Monument → Shrine
```
Start generating Culture and Faith in city 2 immediately.

**Research:** If Animal Husbandry is done, research **Astrology** — but only commit to building the Holy Site district when the Eureka fires (Natural Wonder discovered). If no Natural Wonder has been found yet, build the tech at normal speed; the Holy Site can wait.

---

## Turn 18–25: Pantheon

Pantheons trigger automatically at 25 Faith. With a Shrine running and Desert tiles generating, you'll reach this naturally without a dedicated Faith push.

**Choose:**
```
4+ Desert tiles in your borders?
  YES → Desert Folklore (+1 Faith per Desert tile) — always correct here
Multiple Quarry resources?
  YES → Stone Circles (+2 Faith per Quarry)
Dense Pastures?
  YES → God of the Open Sky (+1 Culture per Pasture)
Targeting Stonehenge or Pyramids?
  YES → Monument to the Gods (+15% Production for Ancient/Classical wonders)
Default:
  → Desert Folklore even with 2–3 Desert tiles; Arabia's start bias makes it reliable
```

> Popular Pantheons disappear fast. Have your choice ready before the trigger fires.

---

## Turn 20–30: Astrology and the Holy Site Question

### When to Build the Holy Site

The Holy Site should be built **when the Astrology Eureka fires** (Natural Wonder discovered) — not on a fixed turn.

**If your Scout found a Natural Wonder:** Queue Astrology now; it costs half as much. Build the Holy Site immediately when the tech finishes.

**If no Natural Wonder found yet:** Continue building Settlers and Campuses. Let the Scout keep looking. The Holy Site can wait until T35–45 in the capital without hurting your strategy — religion is guaranteed.

**If T40 arrives with no Natural Wonder found:** Pay full Astrology cost and build the Holy Site anyway. Don't wait past T45 in the capital.

### Holy Site Placement (When You Do Build It)

Spend a turn comparing tiles before placing:

| Feature | Holy Site Bonus |
|---------|----------------|
| Each adjacent Mountain | +1 Faith |
| Adjacent Natural Wonder | +2 Faith |
| Every 2 adjacent Forest/Woods | +0.5 Faith |
| Adjacent Rainforest | Only with Sacred Path pantheon — not a default bonus |

**Target: +3 or higher.** +4 is strong. Hover over candidate tiles — the game shows the adjacency preview.

> **The Mountain Rule:** A Mountain adjacent to both your Holy Site AND your Campus contributes +1 Faith (Holy Site) and +1 Science (Campus). Plan both districts around the same Mountain cluster.

### Campus Placement — More Important Than the Holy Site

The Madrasa generates Faith equal to its Campus adjacency. Plan your Campus location *before* placing the Holy Site, so both can share the same Mountain cluster.

| Campus Adjacency | Madrasa Faith Output |
|-----------------|---------------------|
| +1 | +1 Faith/turn |
| +2 | +2 Faith/turn |
| +4 | +4 Faith/turn |
| +6 | +6 Faith/turn |

Natural Philosophy (Renaissance civic) doubles this. Plan for +3 or higher Campus adjacency in your core cities.

---

## Turn 22–32: City 2 Active

City 2 is now producing. Check:
- Monument underway (Culture)
- Shrine queued next (Faith)
- Campus placement identified for when Education is researched

**Capital production at this stage:**
```
[Settler done] → Shrine (capital, if not yet built) → [Holy Site when Astrology ready]
```

Or, if Astrology is already done: queue the Holy Site district in the capital now.

**Warrior management:** Move your Warrior(s) to cover both cities. Barbarian camps are most dangerous in the T20–T40 window.

---

## Turn 25–40: City 3

Settle a third city by **Turn 40–45**. Three cities is the minimum for a functional Arabia game.

**Third city criteria:**
- Campus adjacency (Mountains) even more important than in city 2
- Horses accessible somewhere in your empire
- Not too close to the first two cities

**Production in capital at this stage:**
```
[Shrine done] → [Holy Site district if not started] → Temple → Settler for city 3
```

**Research path:**
- After Astrology: **Writing** (unlocks Library; needed for Campus and trading later)
- Then **Bronze Working** (Iron — defensive units, also needed for some wonders)

---

## Turn 35–50: Religion Founded

Great Prophet timing depends on your Faith generation and competition. With the Last Prophet guarantee, you'll found a religion sometime in this window — or by T60–80 at the latest.

**When the Great Prophet appears:**
1. Move it to your capital
2. Use "Found Religion"
3. Choose beliefs carefully — but don't panic about what's left

### Belief Selection

Check what remains. AI civs pick Beliefs suboptimally even at Deity. Both top picks are frequently still available to a late-arriving Arabia.

**Follower Belief:**
```
Jesuit Education available?
  YES → Take it (#1 priority — enables Faith-purchasing Madrasas)
  NO  → Feed the World (+3 Food, +1 Housing from Shrines/Temples)
        OR Religious Community (+1 Production per 2 followers)
```

**Founder Belief:**
```
Tithe available?
  YES → Take it (+1 Gold per 4 followers — scales enormously)
  NO  → Church Property (+2 Gold per following city)
```

> If both Jesuit Education and Tithe are taken: Feed the World + Church Property still works. Don't panic.

### Immediately After Founding

**Deploy Worship Buildings** in every city that has a Temple:
- Costs ~16 Production each (90% discount — Arabia only)
- Takes 1–3 turns per city
- Provides +10% Science, Faith, Culture city-wide
- This is the Vizier's payoff moment — do it immediately

## Turn 50 State Check

Pause and assess. This is where you should be:

| Item | Target | If Missing |
|------|--------|------------|
| Cities settled | 3 (city 4 in progress) | Expand immediately; you're behind |
| Capital Holy Site + Shrine + Temple | ✓ | Build now; you've lost Faith income |
| Religion founded | ✓ or Prophet imminent | Acceptable if 1–2 Prophets unclaimed |
| Worship Building in capital | ✓ | Queue now — 1–3 turns |
| Pantheon chosen | ✓ | Should be done; check Faith income |
| Horses located | ✓ | Research Animal Husbandry if not done |
| Campuses planned in 2+ cities | ✓ | Identify Mountain clusters now |
| No existential military threat | ✓ | Address before expansion |

**What to research at Turn 50:**
- **Education** (unlocks Campus district — your next major infrastructure push)
- Or **Currency** if no Commercial Hub yet

**What comes next:** [Early Game (T50–T80) →](early-game.md) — Campuses, Mamluks, and the transition to the mid-game conquest window.

## Common Turn 1–50 Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Rushing Holy Site before T25 | Sacrifices Settler production that compounds for 100+ turns | Build Settler first; Holy Site when Astrology Eureka fires |
| Researching Astrology before Animal Husbandry | Don't know where Horses are; Mamluk timing delayed | Animal Husbandry first — reveals Horses |
| Placing Holy Site without checking Campus adjacency | Both districts need Mountains; poor placement wastes synergy | Plan Campus location before placing Holy Site |
| Settling only 2 cities by T40 | Not enough production base for Mamluks and Madrasas | Push to 3 cities by T45 |
| Ignoring Worship Building after founding | Missing nearly-free +10% Science/Faith/Culture per city | Deploy empire-wide within the first few turns of founding |
| Waiting for Beliefs before founding | Great Prophet is already available — just choose | Check what's left; make a decision; Tithe and Jesuit are usually still available |
