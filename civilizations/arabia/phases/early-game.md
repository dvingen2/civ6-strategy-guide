# Early Game — Turns 1–80

*Arabia / Saladin Vizier baseline. Sultan variants noted where they differ.*

*[Overview](../overview.md) | [Mid Game →](mid-game.md)*

---

## Phase Summary

The early game sets up Arabia's entire strategy. Your three goals:

1. **Expand** — 3–4 cities by T80, each with solid Campus adjacency identified
2. **Build infrastructure** — Campuses, Monuments, Commercial Hubs; production before faith
3. **Set up the Mamluk window** — Horses located, tech path to Medieval clear

Religion will arrive **on its own**. The Last Prophet guarantee means you can invest your early Production elsewhere and found a religion in the mid-game at no cost to your infrastructure. This is what separates Arabia from every other religious civ — and the single most important thing to internalise about this civ.

## Turn 1–15: First Decisions

### Starting Location: Stay or Settle?

Before pressing Next Turn on Turn 1, assess your starting tile:

**Stay and settle if:**
- [ ] River adjacency for Housing and district bonuses
- [ ] Mountains within 3 hexes — these feed both Campus and Holy Site adjacency later
- [ ] Desert tiles visible for Desert Folklore Faith income
- [ ] Oasis or Floodplain nearby for food security

**Move 1–3 tiles before settling if:**
- [ ] A clearly better river or hill position is visible
- [ ] Moving 1 tile puts Mountains within Campus adjacency range
- [ ] A coastal position is accessible AND river + Mountains are visible inland

> **Rule of thumb:** A 1-tile move that adds +2 Campus adjacency is almost always worth it. Don't move 3+ tiles chasing uncertain improvement.

### Turn 1–2: Scout or Warrior?
Move your starting Warrior to scout immediately while you build. Key information to gather:
- Natural Wonders nearby (Astrology Eureka → triggers Holy Site timing)
- Barbarian camp locations
- City-states (Suzerainty targets)
- Rival civ starting positions

### Turn 1–5: First Build
```
Default:  Scout (or second Warrior)
```
Intelligence about your surroundings compounds the entire game. The one exception: if Barbarian camps are visible close by, build a Warrior first.

## Turn 15–30: Settlers and Expansion

### Priority: Settlers Before Holy Sites

This is the key early-game adjustment for Arabia compared to other religious civs.

Settler production compounds:
- More cities → more Campuses → more Science and Madrasa Faith
- More cities → more Worship Buildings later (deployed in 1–3 turns each, near-free)
- More cities → more Holy Sites (even low-adjacency ones contribute Shrine Faith)

Holy Sites don't need to be rushed because the Prophet is guaranteed. Two extra cities settled before T50 are worth far more than a T30 Holy Site in your capital.

### When to Settle City 2
Aim for T12–20. Earlier if you have an obvious expansion site. Later only if Barbarians are threatening.

### What Makes a Good Second City
1. Mountains accessible for Campus adjacency (≥2 Mountains within 3 hexes of a Campus spot)
2. River access for Housing and Commercial Hub
3. Horses visible (Animal Husbandry reveals them) — Mamluks need Horses
4. Minimum 4 tiles from capital, ideally 5–6 to avoid tile overlap
5. Not exposed to early aggression

### City 2 Build Order
```
Monument → [Shrine] → Campus or Commercial Hub
```
Skip the immediate Shrine in city 2 if Production is tight. A Shrine is worth ~1–2 Faith/turn. A Campus Library is worth ~4–6 Science/turn. Prioritise accordingly.

## Turn 20–50: Campuses and Pantheon

### Pantheon

Pantheons are claimed at 25 Faith — you'll reach this naturally through early Shrines or Desert Folklore tiles without needing a dedicated Faith rush.

**Decision tree:**
```
Do you have 4+ Desert tiles in your territory?
  YES → Desert Folklore [E] — don't deviate
  NO  → Do you have multiple Quarries?
          YES → Stone Circles [S]
        Do you have dense Pastures?
          YES → God of the Open Sky [S]
        Targeting Stonehenge or Pyramids?
          YES → Monument to the Gods [S]
        Default:
          → Desert Folklore (even 2–3 Desert tiles = good value)
```

### Campus Placement — Arabia's Most Important City-Planning Decision

The Madrasa generates Faith equal to its Campus adjacency. A Campus adjacent to 4 Mountains produces +5 Science (Madrasa base) AND +4 Faith/turn. This is why Campus placement matters more than Holy Site placement for the Vizier.

**Campus adjacency checklist:**
- [ ] Mountains adjacent (+1 Science each — primary driver)
- [ ] Rainforest adjacent (+0.5 per 2 tiles)
- [ ] Geothermal Fissures (+2 — rare but very high value)
- [ ] No overlap with tiles needed for Holy Site placement

**Target: +3 or higher Campus adjacency.** +4 is strong. +6 is exceptional.

### Holy Site Placement — When to Build It

The Holy Site is **not** an early-game priority. Build it when:
1. The **Astrology Eureka fires** (discover a Natural Wonder → halves Astrology tech cost → then queue Holy Site)
2. OR by T45–55 at latest in the capital even without the Eureka

If you're choosing between building a Holy Site and building a Settler, build the Settler. The religion will come regardless.

**Holy Site adjacency checklist:**
- [ ] Mountains adjacent (+1 Faith each)
- [ ] Natural Wonder adjacent (+2 Faith — if one is nearby, place adjacent)
- [ ] Rainforest adjacent — only adds Faith with Sacred Path pantheon (not default)

**Target: +4 or higher Holy Site adjacency.** +2 is workable. +1 or less — reconsider the district location.

> **The Mountain Rule:** A Mountain adjacent to both your Campus AND your Holy Site contributes +1 Science (Campus) AND +1 Faith (Holy Site). Plan both districts around the same Mountain cluster where possible.

## Turn 40–70: Religion Arrives

### Great Prophet Mechanics

Seven Great Prophets exist per game. They are claimed in order as civs accumulate enough Faith. Arabia's Last Prophet guarantee means: if Arabia hasn't founded a religion when the final Prophet is claimed, Arabia automatically receives it.

**You do not need to race for a Prophet.** You will get one. The question is whether the best Beliefs are still available when you arrive — and community experience confirms AI civs pick Beliefs suboptimally at all difficulty levels. Jesuit Education and Tithe are frequently still available to a late-claiming Arabia.

### Founding Your Religion

When the Great Prophet appears, use "Found Religion." Select:
1. Pantheon (already chosen — carried forward)
2. **Founder Belief** — your exclusive bonus
3. **Follower Belief** — available to all cities following your religion

**Priority selection:**
```
Founder Belief:   Tithe [E]            (fallback: Church Property)
Follower Belief:  Jesuit Education [E] (fallback: Feed the World)
```

Check what remains before panicking — both top choices are often still available.

### Immediately After Founding
The Vizier's ability makes Worship Buildings near-free (~16 Production). Deploy them in every city within the next few turns. Each Worship Building adds +10% Science, Faith, and Culture city-wide. This is the payoff for your earlier infrastructure investment — more developed cities mean bigger absolute gains from the 10%.

## Turn 50–80: Completing the Foundation

### City 3 and City 4

Settle cities 3 and 4 before T80. These cities form your productive core for the rest of the game.

**Expansion pace:**
```
T12–20: City 2
T30–45: City 3
T50–65: City 4 (if safe and feasible)
```

At Deity difficulty: AI settles very fast. Prioritise city 3 before T45 or lose key sites.

Each expansion city should have:
- A Campus location with ≥2 adjacency identified before settling
- River access for Housing
- Horses accessible somewhere in your empire (for Mamluks)

### Sultan Variant
Sultan has no Worship Building discount — so religion is less of a deployment event. Instead, plan early Classical-era military for a potential T35–50 Classical push to claim land before Mamluks arrive. See [Sultan guide →](saladin-sultan.md).

## Early Game Decision Trees

### "Should I build a Settler or start my Holy Site?"
```
Have I settled 3+ cities yet?
  NO → Build the Settler
Has the Astrology Eureka fired (Natural Wonder discovered)?
  YES → Now build the Holy Site
Am I past T45 with no Holy Site started?
  YES → Build it now regardless of Eureka
```

### "Should I go to war early?"
```
Am I being attacked?
  YES → Defend; build Warrior(s) and walls
Is a weak neighbour blocking my best expansion corridor?
  YES (Sultan) → Consider a Classical push with Warriors + Catapult
  YES (Vizier) → Settle around them or negotiate; wait for Mamluks
Do I have Mamluks yet?
  NO → Avoid costly offensive wars
```

### "City-state nearby — should I prioritise Envoys?"
```
Is it Kandy (Religious)?
  YES → Strong priority — +2 Amenities per Holy Site scales well
Is it Bologna (Scientific)?
  YES → High priority — +1 Great Scientist per Campus
Is it Nazca (Trade/Faith)?
  YES → Moderate priority if you have multiple Campuses near passable features
Is it Military or Cultural?
  SITUATIONAL → Only if directly relevant to current goal
```

## Build Order Templates

### Template A: Mountain Start (Strong Campus + Holy Site potential)
```
Turn 1:    Scout
Turn 5:    Monument
Turn 12:   Settler → City 2
Turn 18:   Shrine (capital — start Faith accumulation)
Turn 28:   Settler → City 3 OR Campus (if Production is strong)
Turn 35:   Holy Site (district in capital — after Astrology Eureka or by T45)
Turn 45:   Temple
Turn 55:   Worship Building (deploy immediately after founding religion)
Turn 60:   Campus → Library in 2–3 cities
```

### Template B: Plains / River Start (Lower adjacency potential)
```
Turn 1:    Scout
Turn 5:    Monument
Turn 15:   Settler → City 2 (find better Campus location)
Turn 22:   Shrine (capital)
Turn 30:   Settler → City 3
Turn 40:   Holy Site (when Astrology Eureka fires or by T50)
Turn 50:   Temple
Turn 55:   Worship Building (post-religion)
Turn 60:   Commercial Hub + Campus in core cities
```

## What to Check at T80

Before the mid-game begins, verify:

- [ ] 3–4 cities settled
- [ ] Religion founded (or Prophet imminent)
- [ ] Worship Buildings deployed in all cities with a Temple
- [ ] Campuses under construction in 2+ core cities
- [ ] Horses located and accessible
- [ ] No unaddressed military threat
- [ ] Theocracy unlocked or on civic path

If more than 2 boxes are unchecked, identify the bottleneck. It's usually either Production (not enough cities yet) or falling into Holy Site rushing at the expense of Settlers.

## Early Game Threats and Responses

| Threat | Response |
|--------|----------|
| Barbarian camp nearby | Build a second Warrior; clear for XP and free Builder |
| Aggressive AI neighbour | 2 Warriors + defensive terrain; send Delegation to open dialogue |
| Another civ racing religion fast | Accept later Beliefs; Last Prophet guarantee holds — check what remains |
| No Natural Wonder found | Queue Astrology without the Eureka and pay full cost; still worth building |
| No Horses on your land | Trade for them or identify them on a neighbouring continent; plan accordingly |
