# Late Game — Turns 150+

*Arabia / Saladin Vizier baseline.*

*[← Mid Game](mid-game.md) | [Victory Paths →](../victory-paths.md)*

---

## Phase Summary

The late game is about closing out your victory condition before opponents can stop you. Arabia has two dominant late-game paths:

- **Religious Victory**: Convert the final holdouts; protect your religion from theological collapse
- **Science Victory**: Push the Space Race to completion

By T150, you should be committed to one. Hedging between two victories in the late game usually results in neither.

---

## Religious Victory — Closing the Game

### The 51% Threshold

Religious Victory triggers when **your religion is the majority religion in 51% of all other civs' cities** at the end of any turn.

At a 6-player game with ~6 cities each (30 rival cities), you need 16 cities under your religion. At 8 players, you need 22+. Count regularly.

### Late-Game Spread Strategy

**Tier 1 — Easy conversions** (do these first):
- City-states (usually only 1 city; 3 Missionary spreads = converted)
- Isolated civs with few cities and no strong religion
- Civs that already follow your religion (spreading to their remaining unconverted cities)

**Tier 2 — Medium difficulty:**
- Civs with a different but weak religion
- Cities far from rival religious "cores"
- Cities connected to your civ via Open Borders/Trade Routes

**Tier 3 — Hard conversions:**
- Civs with strong competing religions (Spain, Russia at full spread)
- Cities deep inside a rival religious civ's territory
- Cities with high religious pressure from multiple directions

**Strategy for Tier 3:**
1. Use Apostles with **Proselytizer** — removes all religions instantly, 2 charges
2. Stack 3+ Missionaries on a city simultaneously for overwhelming pressure
3. Theological Combat: place Debater Apostles adjacent to incoming Missionaries
4. Consider a **Declaration of Holy War** — no diplomatic penalty, opens their borders for spreading

### The Endgame Apostle Corps

By T160–180, you should be Faith-purchasing 1–2 Apostles per turn. Deploy them in waves:
```
Wave 1: Convert easy targets (city-states, weak civs) → 15–20 cities
Wave 2: Contest medium targets with stacked pressure → push to 30+ cities
Wave 3: Proselytizer-equipped Apostles clean stubborn Tier 3 cities → 51%+
```

**Faith budget for late-game spreading:**
- Apostle cost: ~200–300 Faith (scales with era)
- Missionary cost: ~100–150 Faith
- Target: 30–50+ Faith/turn surplus above maintenance

### Protecting Your Religion

While you push outward, defend your home cities:
- Keep 1–2 Apostles in border cities facing strong rival religions
- If a city drops below 60% your religion, actively re-spread before it flips
- **Grand Inquisitors** (purchased with Faith) can remove all other religions from a city — use strategically near borders

### Diplomatic Support for Religious Victory

- Maintain Open Borders agreements with as many civs as possible — spreads your religion passively
- Civs following your religion should be at peace — don't declare war on them needlessly
- Use the World Congress to pass resolutions benefiting your religion when possible

## Science Victory — Space Race

### Space Race Projects (Sequential)

You must complete these projects in order, in a city with a Space Port (Industrial Zone building, unlocked at Rocketry tech):

1. **Launch Earth Satellite** — unlocks at Satellites tech
2. **Launch Moon Landing** — unlocks at Nuclear Fission tech
3. **Launch Mars Colony** — 3 sub-projects (requires Robotics and more)
4. **Launch Exoplanet Expedition** (optional DLC victory step)

> **One city only:** Your fastest-producing city (highest Production + Space Port) should complete all Space Race projects. Don't spread them across cities.

### Science Output Benchmarks by Turn

| Turn | Science/Turn Target | Notes |
|------|--------------------|----|
| T100 | 50+ | 3–4 Madrasas online |
| T130 | 80–100+ | Research Labs being built |
| T160 | 150+ | Full campus network with policies |
| T180 | 200+ | Pushing Space Race techs |

### Science Policy Stack (Democracy era)

Switch to Democracy by T150 for the Science Victory:
```
Economic:   Rationalism (+1 Science per Campus — massive with many Campuses)
Economic:   Five-Year Plan (Production boost — speeds Space Race)
Wildcard:   Research Agreements (+2 Science from all Trade Routes to civs)
Wildcard:   Nobel Prize (+6 Great Scientist points per turn)
```

### Great Scientists — Late-Game Priority

| Scientist | Boost | Era |
|-----------|-------|-----|
| **Albert Einstein** | +250 Science; Research Lab boost | Modern |
| **Alan Turing** | Boosts Computers; speeds research dramatically | Modern |
| **Abdus Salam** | +4 Science from Nuclear Reactor | Atomic |
| **Erwin Schrödinger** | Unlocks/boosts late-game techs | Atomic |

Great Scientists become increasingly scarce — compete hard for them through Campus adjacency and policy cards.

## Late-Game Threats and Responses

### Military Pressure
Late-game civs may declare war to disrupt your victory:
- Mechanized Infantry and Tanks are the main threats
- Maintain an army of at least 4–6 modern units on each border
- Artillery and anti-tank units are defensive investments
- Walls (Walls upgrades via techs) buy time

> You don't need to be the military leader — just not the weakest. Arabia's economic engine should fund an adequate deterrent.

### Rival Religions Surging
If a competing religion threatens your target percentage:
- Prioritise a **Declaration of Holy War** against the spreading civ
- War disrupts their Apostle production and lets you counter-spread
- Peace agreement can include clauses that limit religious activity

### Science Runaway (Rival)
If a Science civ (Korea, Babylon) is running away:
- Check their Space Port location — consider a targeted strike on that city
- Alternatively, push your own Science Victory faster with faith-purchased buildings
- Arabia's dual Religion+Science means you may be close behind even if it seems like they're ahead

## Age Transitions and Dark/Golden Ages

### Targeting Golden Ages

Each era, accumulate **Historic Moments** to earn an Age bonus:
- **Golden Age**: +1 Loyalty to all cities; free Governor title; bonus varies
- **Heroic Age**: Strongest — if coming from a Dark Age. Choose 3 Legacy Bonuses
- **Dark Age**: -Loyalty, but unlocks powerful Legacy Bonuses in the next era

**Arabia's best Golden Age Legacy Bonus:**
- **Free Inquiry** (Science path): +100% Campus adjacency during the era
- **Monumentality** (expansion): +30% Production on districts for the era

### Common Historic Moments (easy to trigger)
- First city of an era to reach a population milestone
- Building specific wonders
- Founding religions (you already have this)
- Researching technologies first (less likely at Deity)
- Building districts in a new city

> Track your Historic Moment score mid-era. If you're close to Dark Age, consider deliberately achieving 1–2 more moments (settle a city, build a district).

## Turn 150–200 Priority Order

```
RELIGION VICTORY PATH:
  1. Faith-purchase Apostles (Proselytizer) every 2–3 turns
  2. Convert Tier 1 → Tier 2 → Tier 3 targets in sequence
  3. Maintain 30–50 Faith/turn surplus
  4. Defend home borders with 1 Apostle per contested city
  5. Monitor % religious coverage each turn — adjust focus as needed

SCIENCE VICTORY PATH:
  1. Research: Scientific Theory → Electricity → Computers → Satellites
  2. Build Space Port in highest-Production city
  3. Faith-purchase Research Labs + Science buildings
  4. Stack Science policy cards in Democracy
  5. Compete for Great Scientists
  6. Begin Space Race projects as soon as each tech unlocks
```

## Turn 200+: Victory Sprint

### Religious Victory
- You need 51% of all rival cities — count carefully
- Diplomacy: Offer Open Borders to civs you haven't reached yet
- If within 5 cities of 51%: push all Apostles simultaneously on remaining targets
- World Congress: Pass "Defender of the Faith" or similar to boost religious spread

### Science Victory
- Space Race is sequential — maintain max Production in your Space Port city
- Do not build non-essential things in your Space Port city during final projects
- Emergency Faith-purchase any remaining Science buildings that would boost output
- Keep the Space Port city defended — it's a target

### Signs You're Close to Victory
**Religion**: The "Victory Progress" screen shows your religion as #1 with 45%+ coverage
**Science**: You've completed Moon Landing and are in Mars Colony sub-projects
