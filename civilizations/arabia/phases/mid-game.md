# Mid Game — Turns 80–150

*Arabia / Saladin Vizier baseline.*

*[← Early Game](early-game.md) | [Late Game →](late-game.md)*

---

## Phase Summary

The mid game is Arabia's **power spike**. Your infrastructure is built, your religion is founded, and the Mamluk arrives. Three things happen in parallel, but one is primary:

1. **Mamluk conquest** `[E]` — this is Arabia's mid-game centrepiece; use T80–130 to acquire territory, cities, and enemy infrastructure
2. **Science scaling** `[H]` — Madrasas deploy across core cities; Natural Philosophy doubles output
3. **Religion spreading** `[H]` — Worship Buildings are already deployed; active Missionary/Apostle campaigns extend coverage

If the early game was investment, the mid game is returns. The Mamluk window does not last forever — use it.

---

## Turn 80–100: The Transition Period

At T80, your empire should look like:
- 3–4 cities with Holy Sites (some with Temples)
- Religion founded; Worship Buildings in capital and expanding
- Theocracy researched or imminent
- First Mamluks either queued or produced

### Immediate Priorities at T80

**Switch to Theocracy** as soon as Reformed Church is researched:
- Enables Faith-purchasing military units
- 15% discount on all Faith purchases (units, buildings, Missionaries)
- +5 Combat Strength for your religious units in Theological Combat
- Policy slots open for religious and economic cards

**Queue Worship Buildings immediately** across your empire:
- Each costs ~16 Production — done in 2–4 turns per city
- Deploy them in *all* cities simultaneously (they're that cheap)
- The yield boost from multiple Worship Buildings compounds fast

**Begin Missionary production** (or Faith-purchase them):
- Missionaries are your spreading tool for willing/neutral cities
- Target nearby city-states and distant AI cities first (easier conversion)
- 2–3 active Missionaries is the minimum; 4–5 is comfortable

### Policy Cards at Theocracy
Review and update:
```
Economic:  Triangular Trade (+4 Gold, +1 Faith per Trade Route)
Economic:  Market Economy or Ilkum (situational)
Military:  Chivalry (+7 Combat Strength to Medieval cavalry = Mamluks)
Wildcard:  Scripture (+100% Holy Site Faith during Classical/Medieval)
Wildcard:  Simultaneity (Faith-purchase units — requires Theocracy)
```

## Turn 90–120: Mamluk Era

### Producing Mamluks

Mamluks unlock at Stirrups (Technology). Build them in cities with:
- High Production (Workshops, Industrial Zones if available)
- Access to Horses (visible on map; improved by Builder)
- Encampment district (Barracks gives +1 Combat XP per turn)

**Target:** 4–6 Mamluks by T110. This is enough to threaten any neighbor and defend all borders.

### What to Do With Mamluks

#### Option A: Targeted Conquest (Recommended for all victory paths)

Even a Religious or Science Victory benefits from Mamluk conquest: conquered cities mean more Worship Building multipliers, more Campuses, more followers for Tithe. Arabia's mid-game advantage exists right now — don't waste it on pure defense.

Best candidates for conquest:
- A city with **Mountain adjacency** you can't settle (already claimed)
- A neighbor who is **spreading a competing religion** aggressively
- A city-state you want to absorb for its specialty bonus

**Conquest rules with Mamluks:**
- Always bring 1–2 Ranged units (Crossbowmen) alongside Mamluks
- Mamluks heal passively — keep them in the front, injured is fine
- Use Battering Ram (if walls present) or Siege Tower to breach city walls
- Once a city falls, leave 1–2 Mamluks garrisoned; convert with Apostle immediately

#### Option B: Defensive Only (Sub-optimal)
- Keep Mamluks on your borders as deterrent only
- Only if you have no viable conquest targets or are diplomatically constrained
- You are leaving territory and infrastructure on the table

#### Option C: Opportunistic (Hybrid)
- Respond to threats as they arise
- Use Mamluks to punish civs that spread rival religions into your territory
- Raiding for Gold and tech when ahead militarily

## Turn 100–130: Religion Campaign

### Spreading Strategy

By T100–110, your religion should hold all of your own cities. Now push outward:

**Priority targets:**
1. **Nearby City-States** — easy conversion; unlocks suzerainty bonuses
2. **Close AI civs** — easier spreading range; improves diplomatic relations
3. **Civs with high city counts** — more followers = more Tithe Gold

**Spreading mechanics:**
- **Missionaries**: 3 charges of spreading (+10 pressure per use); ~100 Faith to produce
- **Apostles**: 2–3 charges; stronger spread; can engage in Theological Combat
- **Trade Routes**: Passively spread your religion to destination cities each turn

### Apostle Promotions to Prioritize
When Apostles gain XP from spreading or Theological Combat:

| Promotion | Use Case |
|-----------|---------|
| **Proselytizer** | Removes all other religions from the city — critical for contested cities |
| **Translator** | Spreads to 2 additional cities adjacent to target |
| **Debater** | +10 Combat strength in Theological Combat |
| **Heathen Conversion** | Converts Barbarians to units — situational but powerful |

**Corps suggestion:** Keep 2 Apostles dedicated to offense (pushing into rival religious territory) and 1–2 for defense (protecting your border cities from counter-spreading).

### Dealing with Rival Religions

If another civ is spreading their religion into your territory:
1. Check city's dominant religion each turn — a city below 50% your religion is at risk
2. Position an Apostle (Debater) in that city to engage arriving enemy Apostles/Missionaries
3. Re-spread aggressively into the contested city
4. Consider a declaration of war if the civ is actively proselytizing with many units (a formal "Declaration of Holy War" causes no diplomatic penalties)

## Turn 110–140: Science Engine Deployment

### Madrasa Rollout

Education research is complete (or near complete) by this phase. Now:

1. Build Campus in every city with ≥+2 Mountain adjacency available
2. Faith-purchase Madrasas via Jesuit Education (if chosen)
3. Also build the Library first in each Campus for the +2 Science base

**Madrasa faith cost** (approximate): ~200 Faith. With strong Faith generation (4+ cities with Holy Sites, Worship Buildings, Triangular Trade), you should be producing 30–60 Faith/turn by T120, meaning 1 Madrasa every 3–7 turns via Faith purchase.

### Great Scientist Targeting
From T110 onward, compete for Great Scientists:
- Campus districts generate +1 Great Scientist point/turn each
- Madrasa generates +1 Great Scientist point/turn (same as University)
- Target early Great Scientists for campus bonuses and research boosts

Key Great Scientists for Arabia:
| Scientist | Bonus | Era |
|-----------|-------|-----|
| **Euclid** | Campus buildings provide +1 Science/turn | Classical |
| **Isaac Newton** | Unlocks Natural Philosophy; boosts to Physics/Scientific Theory | Medieval |
| **Charles Darwin** | +2 Science from each Natural Wonder | Medieval |
| **Janaki Ammal** | Campus tiles yield +1 Science | Industrial |

## Turn 120–150: Mid-Game Assessment

### Religious Victory Check
At T130, assess:
- What % of all cities follow your religion?
- Are you spreading faster than rivals?
- Do you have diplomatic access (Open Borders) to civs that need converting?

**You're on track if:**
- 40%+ of all cities follow your religion by T130
- You have 4+ active Missionaries/Apostles
- No single rival religion dominates more than 30% of cities

**You're behind if:**
- A rival religion has spread into your own core cities
- You lack Faith income to sustain Apostle production
- Spreading range is too short to reach distant civs

**Correction actions:**
- Build/faith-purchase more Missionaries (quantity beats quality for coverage)
- Switch Trade Routes to target unconverted civs
- Negotiate Open Borders to speed spread access
- Consider Holy War if a civ is actively destroying your religion

### Science Victory Check (if pivoting)
At T130, assess your Science output:
- Are Madrasas deployed in your 3+ best cities?
- Is Science/turn ≥ 100 per turn? (Rough benchmark for late-game competitiveness)
- Are you researching Industrial-era techs?

## Mid-Game Policy Card Audit

Review policies at each government change and each era shift:

### Theocracy (your primary government)
```
Best configuration at T100–130:
  Econ 1:  Triangular Trade (+4 Gold +1 Faith per Trade Route)
  Econ 2:  Market Economy or Serfdom (+1 Builder charge)
  Military: Chivalry (Mamluks get +7 CS)
  Wildcard 1: Simultaneity (Faith-buy units)
  Wildcard 2: Scripture (if still in Medieval era) OR Raj (if suzerain of CS)
```

### Consider Democracy (from Industrial era)
Once Theocracy's benefits are less critical (Mamluks are replaced, religion is spreading fine):
- Democracy offers powerful Science and Culture cards
- Rationalism (+1 Science per Campus district) becomes significant
- Switch when your Science Victory becomes the primary focus

## Wonders: Mid-Game Targets

Wonders are powerful but compete with district time. Prioritise only if you're ahead on districts.

| Wonder | Benefit | Priority |
|--------|---------|---------|
| **Grand Mosque of Djenne** | Missionaries get +2 spreads; Builders get +1 charge | `[H]` Great for religious spreading |
| **Hagia Sophia** | Missionaries get +1 spread AND your Apostles gain a promotion | `[H]` Religious Victory accelerant |
| **Oxford University** | +20% Science in city; free tech of current era | `[S]` Science Victory support |
| **Big Ben** | +100% Treasury; +1 Economic policy slot | `[S]` Strong if gold-focused |
| **Venetian Arsenal** | Double naval units produced | `[–]` Skip unless island map |

> **Hagia Sophia is Arabia's best mid-game wonder.** Free Apostle promotion + extra Missionary charges = faster, more resilient spreading.
