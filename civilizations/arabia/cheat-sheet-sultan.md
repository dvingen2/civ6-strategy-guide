## Flanking Mechanic (Sultan's Core)

Flanking occurs when you attack an enemy unit **already adjacent to one of your other units**.

**Normal flanking:** small Combat Strength bonus  
**Sultan flanking:** doubled — significantly more damage

```
Setup a flank attack:
  Unit A moves adjacent to enemy
  Unit B attacks from opposite side → FLANKING BONUS (doubled)
```

Always position two Mamluks on opposite sides of a target before attacking. Never waste Sultan's bonus by attacking solo.

## Mamluk Flanking Formula

```
Normal attack:           ~50–60% damage dealt
Flanking attack:         +Combat Strength bonus → more damage
Sultan flanking attack:  +DOUBLED bonus → substantially more damage
```

Stack Chivalry policy (+7 CS), Encampment XP promotions, and flanking angles for maximum output.

## Early War (Classical, Optional)

**When to attack:**
- A weak neighbour has 1–2 cities blocking your best expansion sites
- They have no walls (you can see their city's defence strength)
- You have 3 Warriors + 1 Catapult

**How:**
1. Move Catapult adjacent to their city
2. Bombard walls first (Catapult attacks city from range)
3. Two Warriors flank attack the defending unit (flanking bonus active)
4. Capture when city HP is low

**After capture:** Shrine immediately; send Apostle to convert within 10 turns.

## Religion for Sultan

Sultan's religion is maintained and leveraged, not primary:
- Still found a religion (Last Prophet guarantee)
- Spread to conquered cities to improve Loyalty
- Holy Wars allow war without diplomatic penalty
- Religious Victory still possible as secondary path

```
[ ] 4+ Mamluks operational by T85
[ ] Horses improved and accessible
[ ] Chivalry policy card slotted
[ ] Encampment in military city
[ ] Theocracy → faith-buy Mamluks
```

## When Things Go Wrong

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| No Horses | Trade for them; pivot to Crossbowmen + Siege |
| Conquered city in revolt | Governor + religion spread + Amenities (Stupa) |
| Outpaced militarily in late game | Upgrade units; switch to Fascism; fortify cities |
| Religion being destroyed | Apostle (Proselytizer) on border cities |
| War too costly | Make peace, keep cities gained; rebuild |
