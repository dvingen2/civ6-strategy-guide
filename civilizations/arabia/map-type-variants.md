# Map Type Variants

*How Arabia's strategy shifts on different map types.*

*[Overview →](overview.md) | [Situations →](situations.md)*

---

## Overview

Arabia's core strategy is tuned for land-based play with a desert start bias, Mountain access, and room to expand peacefully before Medieval. Different map types challenge different parts of this.

---

## Continents (Standard / Recommended for Beginners)

**Arabia Suitability: Excellent**

**Characteristics:**
- Large landmasses separated by oceans
- Arabian desert start bias usually triggers naturally
- Enough room to settle 3–4 good cities before neighbours crowd you
- Inter-continental spreading requires naval Missionaries later

**Play as written.** This map type is the baseline for all framework guidance.

**Specific notes:**
- Build a Harbor in at least 1 coastal city for naval Trade Routes and Missionaries
- Late Religious Victory: you'll need to cross water to convert distant civs — build Apostles/Missionaries near your coast and use Embarkation (researched with Sailing) to reach island or other-continent civs
- Theocracy's +CS in theological combat helps your Apostles survive water-based spreading campaigns

---

## Pangaea (Single landmass)

**Arabia Suitability: Good**

**Characteristics:**
- All civs on one large continent
- More early contact — neighbours are close; war risk higher
- No ocean barrier — rival religions spread to you faster
- More Theological Combat; more warmonger pressure

**Adjustments:**
- **Third Warrior before T40** — aggressive AIs can reach you early
- **Apostle defense corps earlier** — rival Missionaries will arrive in your territory faster than on Continents
- **Theocracy government urgency: T75–80** — you need faith-buy units sooner for defense
- Religious spreading is more competitive — rivals are closer to all your targets too
- Holy War is more available (rivals' cities often have different religion from contact)

**Opportunity:** More warmonger targets nearby. Sultan shines on Pangaea — Mamluks can dominate the single landmass.

---

## Archipelago (Many islands)

**Arabia Suitability: Moderate — requires significant adjustment**

**Characteristics:**
- Many small and medium islands
- Desert start bias may not trigger (less Desert terrain on island maps)
- Harbors are mandatory for Trade Routes and unit movement
- Religious spreading requires naval Missionaries the entire game
- Theological Combat happens mostly on water

**Major adjustments:**
- **Desert Folklore may not be available** — pivot to God of the Open Sky, Stone Circles, or Sacred Path
- **Harbor before Commercial Hub** — you need naval access; build Harbor in your capital
- **Sailing tech earlier** (unlocks Embarkation: land units can move on water)
- Campus adjacency may be weaker — islands often have fewer Mountains; plan for lower Madrasa Faith
- Spread religion via Trade Routes heavily — each route spreads to the destination city passively every turn; this matters more than Missionaries on island maps

**Vizier vs Sultan on Archipelago:** Vizier. Sultan's flanking bonuses are land-based; naval warfare in Civ 6 works differently. Vizier's religious spreading via cheap Worship Buildings and Trade Routes is more effective here.

**Victory path shift:** Science > Religious on Archipelago. Converting distant islands is slow; Science Victory has no geographic constraint.

---

## Highland (Many Mountains)

**Arabia Suitability: Exceptional**

**Characteristics:**
- Dense Mountain ranges across the map
- Holy Sites AND Campuses achieve maximum adjacency
- Some paths blocked by Mountains — expansion can be slower

**Play style:** This is Arabia's dream map. Lean into it.

**Adjustments:**
- **Holy Site adjacency targets: +5 or higher** — revise "target +4" upward to +5 minimum on Highland
- **Madrasa Faith will be substantial** — Natural Philosophy doubles this to massive Faith generation
- Campus adjacency +4–6 is achievable early — Madrasas become the primary Faith source
- Expansion is slower due to Mountains blocking movement — use roads and passes
- Fewer flat tiles = less food; build Aqueducts early for Housing

**Opportunity:** Highland maps can generate 50+ Faith/turn from Madrasas alone in the late game — a Religious Victory from Science infrastructure effectively.

---

## Desert (All or Mostly Desert)

**Arabia Suitability: Exceptional — highest synergy**

**Characteristics:**
- Abundant Desert tiles
- Desert Folklore at maximum value
- Fewer trees, more open terrain
- Resources: Oil appears frequently on Desert; Horses may be limited
- Often has Oasis tiles — very high Food value

**Adjustments:**
- **Desert Folklore is mandatory** — never deviate on this map type
- Count Desert tiles before settling — 8+ Desert tiles = enormous Faith income from Pantheon alone
- Oasis tiles are precious: +3 Food, +1 Production, +1 Gold on a single tile; settle adjacent to Oasis clusters
- Campus adjacency may be lower on flat Desert — look for Mountain clusters carefully
- Holy Site may rely more on Oasis adjacency (check if your specific natural wonder is Desert-appropriate)

**Special consideration:** The Petra wonder (Ancient, Masonry) gives +2 Food, +1 Production, +1 Gold to all Desert tiles. If you're on a Desert map, competing for Petra is worth considering — it transforms your Desert tiles into high-yield tiles.

---

## Fractal (Random terrain, unpredictable)

**Arabia Suitability: Variable**

Fractal generates highly variable terrain. You might get a Mountain-heavy start or a flat coastal one.

**Adjust based on your actual start:**
- If Mountain-heavy → play like Highland guide
- If water-heavy → play like Archipelago guide  
- If Desert-heavy → play like Desert guide
- If none of the above → play standard Continents strategy

The adaptability guides in [Situations →](situations.md) are most relevant here.

## Map Size Effects

Map size affects how many civs and city-states appear, which changes Religious Victory difficulty.

| Map Size | Rival Cities to Convert (approx.) | Notes |
|---------|----------------------------------|-------|
| Duel | 5–10 | Easy Religious Victory; few targets |
| Small | 15–25 | Manageable |
| Standard | 25–40 | Framework baseline |
| Large | 40–60 | More Apostles needed; longer spreading campaign |
| Huge | 60–80+ | Religious Victory is hard; consider Science |

**On Huge maps:** Arabia's Religious Victory is achievable but takes more turns and more Apostles. Plan for 20–30 active Missionaries/Apostles in the late game. Alternatively, pursue Science Victory — it has no population-of-map requirement.

## Start Bias Interaction

Arabia has a **Desert start bias** — the game tries to start you near Desert terrain.

**What this usually means:**
- 4–8 Desert tiles within your starting territory
- Oasis tiles possible (1-in-4 Desert starts)
- Mountains often present near Desert terrain (geologically realistic)
- Horses may be scarce on pure Desert starts (spawn in Plains/Grassland more)

**On maps where Desert is scarce** (Tundra, Boreal, Alpine): Your start bias may not trigger properly. If you end up on a non-Desert start:
- Skip Desert Folklore; evaluate other Pantheons
- Adjust city placement to focus on Mountains/Rivers instead of Desert clusters
- The Madrasa/Worship Building strategy still works; you simply lose the Pantheon boost

---

## Quick Map Selection Recommendation

If you're choosing a map for learning Arabia:
1. **Continents, Standard size, 6 civs** — cleanest experience; framework applies exactly as written
2. **Highland** — if you want to see Arabia's full potential (Mountains everywhere)
3. **Desert** — if you want maximum Faith generation for easy Religious Victory

Avoid Archipelago for your first few games — it requires too many adjustments to Arabia's default playstyle.
