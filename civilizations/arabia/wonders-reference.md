# Wonders — Arabia Priority Reference

*Which wonders to build, when, and when to skip.*

*[Policy Cards →](policy-cards-reference.md) | [Vizier Guide →](leaders/saladin-vizier.md)*

---

## How Wonders Work

- Wonders are unique buildings — only **one exists per game**, globally
- Built like a district: costs Production over multiple turns in a single city
- First civ to complete it owns it; all others' Production is lost (wasted turns)
- Most powerful, but heavily contested

**Key trade-off:** Every turn spent building a wonder is a turn not spent on a district or military unit. Don't build wonders just because they're impressive — only build them when they provide something your strategy specifically needs.

## Arabia Wonder Priority Tiers

### Must-Build (when available and not contested)

#### Hagia Sophia `[E]` — Medieval
| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| **Era** | Medieval |
| **Tech required** | Education |
| **Effect** | Missionaries receive +1 Spread charge. Apostles receive a free Promotion on creation. |

**Why Arabia needs it:**
- Missionaries spreading your religion get an extra use (3 charges instead of 2) — that's 50% more spreading per unit Faith spent
- Apostles start with a free promotion — means your Apostle corps is immediately stronger at Theological Combat or spreading

**When to build:** T80–120. Queue it as soon as Education is researched if you're winning Production.

**When to skip:** If another civ is likely to beat you to it (watch the wonder counter), or if you're behind on Holy Sites and need to prioritise district production.

---

#### Grand Mosque of Djenne `[H]` — Medieval
| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| **Era** | Medieval |
| **Tech required** | Divine Right (Civic) |
| **Effect** | +2 Spread charges for all Missionaries. +1 Builder charge for all Builders in this city. |

**Why Arabia wants it:**
- Combined with Hagia Sophia: Missionaries have 4 or 5 spread charges instead of 2 — enormous Religious Victory efficiency
- Builder charges speed up tile improvements across your empire

**Priority note:** Build Hagia Sophia first. If Djenne is still available after that, pursue it.

---

### High Value (build if early enough and not sacrificing districts)

#### Oxford University `[H]` — Industrial
| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| **Era** | Industrial |
| **Tech required** | Scientific Theory |
| **Effect** | +20% Science in the city. 2 free Techs of the current era. |

**Why Arabia wants it (Science path):**
- 2 free techs of the Industrial era is enormous — can skip Electricity or Industrialization, saving 30–50 turns of research
- +20% Science stacks with Rationalism policy and Madrasa output

**Build in:** Your highest-Science city (best Madrasa city).

---

#### Alhambra `[H]` — Medieval
| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| **Era** | Medieval |
| **Tech required** | Castles |
| **Effect** | +2 Military policy slots. Free Armory in this city. All units trained in this city receive a free promotion. |

**Why Arabia wants it:**
- +2 Military policy slots — equip Chivalry AND another military card simultaneously
- All units trained here get a free promotion — Mamluks start with a combat edge
- Essentially free Armory accelerates Mamluk production

**Build in:** Your primary Mamluk production city (your strongest military city).

---

#### Stonehenge `[S]` — Ancient (rush)
| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| **Era** | Ancient |
| **Tech required** | Astrology |
| **Effect** | Found a free Pantheon (if no one has). +6 Faith/turn. |

**Why Arabia might want it:**
- Guarantees your Pantheon choice before anyone else can take it
- +6 Faith/turn is meaningful in the Ancient era
- Requires rushing Production very early

**Reality check:** This is only viable if you have exceptional early Production (many Hills, starting Quarry). Stonehenge is usually gone by Turn 30. Most games: skip it and focus on Holy Site instead.

---

#### Great Bath `[S]` — Ancient
| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| **Era** | Ancient |
| **Tech required** | Pottery |
| **Effect** | +3 Housing. +1 Amenity. Floodplain tiles get +1 Food. Eliminates flooding damage. |

**Why Arabia might want it:** Strong start stabilisation if you settled on a Floodplain-heavy river. Housing from the Ancient era is rare and valuable.

**When to build:** Only if you have very high early Production and you're on a Floodplain-rich start.

---

#### Potala Palace `[S]` — Renaissance
| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| **Era** | Renaissance |
| **Tech required** | Astronomy |
| **Effect** | +1 Diplomatic policy slot. +2 Wildcard policy slots. +1 Culture, +1 Faith per mountain |

**Why Arabia might want it:**
- +2 Wildcard slots is significant — lets you slot Natural Philosophy AND Simultaneity together
- +1 Faith per Mountain in your territory — passive scaling with Arabia's mountain preference

**When to build:** If your game has many Mountains and you can claim it before another civ.

---

### Situational (build only under specific conditions)

#### Big Ben `[S]` — Industrial
| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| **Era** | Industrial |
| **Effect** | +100% Treasury (doubles your current Gold). +1 Economic policy slot. |

**Build when:** You're rolling in Gold from Tithe and Trade Routes and want to leverage it into a massive treasury surge for faith-purchasing a wave of buildings.

---

#### Venetian Arsenal `[–]` — Renaissance
Build only on water-heavy maps. Skip on standard Continents/Pangaea.

### Skip (not worth it for Arabia)

|--------|---------|
| **Colosseum** | Amenity/culture; not your priority |
| **Eiffel Tower** | Tourism/Appeal; not your path |
| **Cristo Redentor** | Doubles National Park tourism; irrelevant |

| **Satellite City** | Not relevant to your production needs |

## Wonder Build Order (Priority Sequence)

```
Ancient:    Stonehenge [ONLY if high Production + good start — otherwise skip]
Medieval:   Hagia Sophia [E] → Grand Mosque of Djenne [H] → Alhambra [H]
Industrial: Oxford University [H]
Renaissance: Potala Palace [S] (if available and Mountain-heavy game)
```

## Competition Awareness

**Wonders other civs chase that conflict with yours:**
- Hagia Sophia — contested by all religious civs. Build it fast or not at all.
- Alhambra — military civs (Aztec, Mongolia) often build it. Check the "Wonder Progress" screen.
- Oxford — Korea and Babylon almost always build this. Compete only if you're ahead in Science.

**How to check wonder competition:**
- Open a rival city's production queue if you have a Spy there, or watch for the Wonder indicator in the notification bar
- The game notifies you when a wonder is completed by another civ — monitor from Turn 60 onward

**Rule:** If you're more than 15 turns behind on building a wonder, abandon it and queue a district instead. Partial wonder production is wasted Production.
