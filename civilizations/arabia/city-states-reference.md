## How City-States Work

City-states are independent single-city civilisations. You can't conquer them (without a major diplomatic penalty) but you can **influence** them through Envoys.

- **Envoys** — earned by researching civics. Assign them to city-states to build influence.
- **Suzerain** — the civ with the most Envoys in a city-state is its Suzerain; gets the unique bonus.
- **Contested** — multiple civs compete for Suzerainty. You need the most Envoys to win.
- **Minimum for bonus tiers:** 1 Envoy = minor yield, 3 = better yield, 6 = full bonus, Suzerain = unique ability

## Arabia Priority City-States

### Tier 1 — Pursue Actively (send Envoys immediately)

#### Kandy (Religious)
| Bonus | Suzerain Ability |
|-------|-----------------|
| +2 Amenities from Holy Sites | Gain Relic of the Patron Saint for each Great Person earned in any city |

**Why Arabia:** +2 Amenities per Holy Site means your religion network passively solves your Amenity problem. Arabia builds many Holy Sites — Kandy's bonus scales with your core strategy. The Suzerain relic generation is secondary.

---

#### Nazca (Religious)
| Bonus | Suzerain Ability |
|-------|-----------------|
| +1 Faith from Passable Features | Builders can construct Nazca Lines (improvements) |

**Why Arabia:** +1 Faith from Desert, Floodplain, and other features — Arabia's desert-heavy start often has these. The passive Faith income is directly valuable.

---

#### La Venta (Religious)
| Bonus | Suzerain Ability |
|-------|-----------------|
| +1 Faith from Improvements | Builders can construct Colossal Heads (improvements) +Faith |

**Why Arabia (early game):** La Venta's suzerainty lets you place Colossal Heads on tiles, each providing +2 Faith. Exceptional early-game Faith generation if you can reach it first.

---

#### Bologna (Scientific)
| Bonus | Suzerain Ability |
|-------|-----------------|
| +1 Great Scientist point per Campus | Grants a free building in all Campuses equal to the current tech era |

**Why Arabia:** +1 Great Scientist point per Campus compounds across your entire Campus network. The free building is powerful — free Libraries, Universities, or Research Labs as you advance eras.

---

#### Geneva (Scientific)
| Bonus | Suzerain Ability |
|-------|-----------------|
| +15 Science per turn | You cannot declare Surprise Wars |

**Why Arabia (Science path):** +15 Science/turn is a significant flat bonus. **Caveat:** Suzerain of Geneva means you cannot declare Surprise Wars — you must declare formal war types only. Manageable for Arabia (use Holy War instead).

---

### Tier 2 — Value When Available

#### Chinguetti (Religious)
| Suzerain Ability |
|-----------------|
| +4 Faith per Trade Route to a Holy City |

**Why Arabia:** If your holy city (capital) receives trade routes, this generates free Faith. Works best when other civs follow your religion and send routes to you.

---

#### Singapore (Trade)
| Suzerain Ability |
|-----------------|
| +2 Production and +4 Gold for all your Trade Routes |

**Why Arabia:** More Gold from Trade Routes compounds with Triangular Trade. Useful from Medieval onward when Trade Routes are numerous.

---

#### Kumasi (Cultural/Trade)
| Suzerain Ability |
|-----------------|
| +2 Culture and +2 Gold for each specialty district in the city |

**Why Arabia:** As you build Holy Sites, Campuses, and Commercial Hubs, Kumasi's per-district bonus multiplies. Strong in a wide empire.

---

#### Hunza (Trade)
| Suzerain Ability |
|-----------------|
| +4 Gold per Trade Route to a foreign city with a Holy Site |

**Why Arabia:** If spreading religion to civs who build Holy Sites, this generates significant Gold income from your trade network.

---

#### Hattusa (Scientific)
| Suzerain Ability |
|-----------------|
| Free technology boost for current era when you gain suzerainty |

**Why Arabia:** A free tech boost can advance you one tech instantly. Most valuable if claimed mid-game when tech costs are high.

---

### Tier 3 — Take If Nearby and Uncontested

#### Granada (Military)
| Suzerain Ability |
|-----------------|
| All newly trained land units receive a free promotion |

**Why Arabia (Sultan):** Mamluks start with a combat promotion — Alhambra wonder does the same, so this stacks.

---

#### Preslav (Military)
| Suzerain Ability |
|-----------------|
| +5 CS to all units adjacent to heavy cavalry |

**Why Arabia:** Direct Mamluk combat bonus. If pursuing Domination (Sultan), this is more valuable.

## Envoy Strategy

### When to Send Envoys

1. **Immediately** when earned — every civic research earns 1+ Envoys; send them the same turn
2. **Prioritise Tier 1 city-states** until you have suzerainty, then move to Tier 2
3. **Defend suzerainty** — if a rival is close to overtaking you, send additional Envoys before they can

### How Many Envoys Does Suzerainty Require?

You need **more Envoys than any other single civ** in that city-state. There's no fixed number — it's a competition.

Common pattern:
- Turn 30–50: 3 Envoys gets suzerainty of most city-states (other civs have 1–2)
- Turn 80–120: May need 6–8 to hold suzerainty as other civs invest more

### Envoy Priority Queue for Arabia

```
T1:   Send all starting Envoys → Kandy or Bologna (whichever is on the map)
T30+: Each new Envoy → defend existing suzerainties first, then expand to Tier 2
T80:  Evaluate which city-states are most contested; accept losses on Tier 3
T120: Focus Envoys on city-states that serve your active victory path
```

---

## Suzerainty Conflicts: What to Do

If another civ steals suzerainty from you:

1. **Is it Tier 1?** — Send 3 Envoys immediately to reclaim it. This is worth doing.
2. **Is it Tier 2?** — Wait until your next civic earns Envoys; reassess.
3. **Is it Tier 3?** — Let it go; invest elsewhere.

> **Don't overinvest Envoys to defend a low-priority city-state** at the expense of your Tier 1 targets.
