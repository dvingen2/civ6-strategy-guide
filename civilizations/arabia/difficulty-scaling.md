# Difficulty Scaling Guide

*How Arabia's strategy changes across difficulty levels.*

*All other files in this framework assume **Prince** unless noted here.*

---

## How Difficulty Works in Civ 6

AI civs receive bonus yields (Production, Science, Culture, Faith, Gold) at higher difficulties. They do not play smarter — they just start with more. This means:

- **Higher difficulty = AIs expand faster, research faster, build armies faster**
- Your strategy doesn't fundamentally change, but **timing must be tighter**
- On Deity, an AI that gets the same Great Prophet as you on Prince will get it 20–30 turns earlier

---

## Difficulty Level Reference

| Difficulty | AI Bonus | What It Means for Arabia |
|-----------|---------|--------------------------|
| **Settler** | Large handicap to player | Almost anything works; use to learn mechanics |
| **Chieftain** | Small handicap to player | Forgiving; good for learning sequences |
| **Warlord** | Slight handicap to player | Introduction to real competition |
| **Prince** | Balanced | Framework baseline. No AI bonuses. |
| **King** | AI: +15% Production/Science/Culture | Tighter timing; AIs settle faster |
| **Emperor** | AI: +30% | AIs are ahead by Turn 50; must be very efficient |
| **Immortal** | AI: +50% | Top 2 Prophet claimed by Turn 40; Beliefs erode fast |
| **Deity** | AI: large bonuses + starting units | Belief selection at risk; military pressure from Turn 1 |

---

## Prince (Baseline)

Everything in the framework applies as written. No adjustments needed.

Arabia is strong at Prince. "Last Prophet" means you don't stress about the Faith race.

---

## King / Emperor

**What changes:**
- AIs settle second and third cities 5–10 turns earlier than you
- Science/Culture race is tighter — AIs may research Religion-blocking techs or civics faster
- Great Prophets are claimed 10–20 turns earlier than at Prince

**Adjustments:**
- **Settle city 2 by Turn 18, not 25.** Every turn you delay, an AI is claiming a good site.
- **Astrology when the Eureka fires** — same approach as Prince; don't rush it at the expense of Settlers
- The infrastructure-first opening matters even more at King/Emperor — the compounding advantage of extra cities is what keeps you competitive
- Holy Site placement is even more important — fewer opportunities to settle Mountain-adjacent sites because AIs claim them

**Belief availability:** Most strong Beliefs are still available. Jesuit Education and Tithe rarely disappear before your Prophet on King/Emperor.

---

## Immortal

**What changes:**
- AIs will claim top 2–3 Prophets before Turn 60 in many games
- Jesuit Education or Tithe may be gone before your Prophet — plan alternatives
- AIs have strong enough militaries to threaten you even after Mamluks arrive
- Border pressure from settled AI cities is real — you have less time to expand peacefully

**Adjustments:**
- **Settler before Shrine** remains correct — extra cities compound faster than early Faith
- **Warrior before Scout** may be appropriate if aggressive AIs are nearby
- **Pantheon by Turn 20–25** — Desert Folklore accumulates naturally without a dedicated rush
- **Holy Site when Astrology Eureka fires** — don't rush it; the Prophet is guaranteed regardless
- **Military required earlier** — 2–3 Warriors ready by Turn 30 or a nearby aggressive AI will pressure you
- **Be ready to adapt Beliefs:** Know your fallback options. If Jesuit Education is taken, plan to produce Madrasas via Production. If Tithe is taken, Church Property becomes the Founder Belief.
- **Theocracy timing is critical** — reach Reformed Church by Turn 80–90 or fall behind the Faith economy

**Prophet targeting at Immortal:**
- Aim for Prophet #4–6 (you may not reach earlier without sacrificing early development)
- "Last Prophet" guarantee remains — even if you get the 7th, your religion exists and works

---

## Deity

**What changes:**
- AIs have significant starting bonuses — militaries are present and threatening from Turn 1
- Great Prophets 1–4 are often claimed before Turn 60
- Beliefs are frequently depleted of top choices by the time you found
- AIs settle extremely aggressively — you may be hemmed in by Turn 30
- Barbarians are set to maximum strength and frequency

**Major adjustments:**

### Opening
- **Warrior first** (not Scout) — Barbarians and aggressive AIs require immediate defense
- Alternatively: **Shrine first** if you're in a protected position and need Faith urgently
- **No delay on city 2** — settle by Turn 15 or lose the site permanently
- Accept that you will explore less — prioritise survival over scouting

### Religion Race
Arabia's Last Prophet guarantee matters even more at Deity: use it deliberately. The infrastructure-first approach — building Settlers, Campuses, and military before Holy Sites — applies at all difficulty levels, and Deity is no exception.

- Use Desert Folklore if at all possible — it's the most reliable early Faith generator with no production cost
- Build Holy Site when the Astrology Eureka fires (Natural Wonder discovered) — not on a fixed turn
- **Beliefs at Deity:** AI civs pick Beliefs suboptimally even at Deity. Jesuit Education and Tithe are frequently still available to a late-arriving Prophet. Check what remains before assuming the worst.
- **If top Beliefs are taken:** Religious Community + Church Property still produces a functional religion. Don't panic — the Worship Building boost works regardless of Belief selection.

The "Last Prophet" strategy — delaying Holy Sites in favour of Production and military — is not a Deity-specific trick. It is the recommended baseline approach across all difficulties. At Deity, the military arguments for it are simply stronger.

### Military
- **2 Warriors + walls by Turn 30** if any aggressive AI is nearby
- Mamluks are still excellent at Deity — but you need them operational by T85, not T100
- Prioritise Stirrups tech earlier (before or alongside Education)
- Consider Encampment in your military city earlier — faster Mamluk production at an early threat

### Expansion
- **3 cities by Turn 50, not Turn 70.** This is very tight but necessary.
- On Deity, every turn you delay settling is a turn an AI is settling in your planned location
- If you get hemmed in (only 2 good city sites): consider an early war to take one good city from a weak AI before they develop it

### Science Victory at Deity
Arabia pursuing Science at Deity is difficult — Korea, Babylon, and other Science civs have massive starting advantages. Unless you have an exceptional Mountain start with many Campuses, focus on Religious Victory at Deity.

## What Never Changes Regardless of Difficulty

- **Holy Site placement** — adjacency math doesn't change with difficulty
- **Madrasa mechanic** — Campus adjacency → Faith regardless of AI bonuses
- **Worship Building near-free cost** — still cheap at all difficulty levels
- **Mamluk healing** — still heals every turn at Deity
- **"Last Prophet" guarantee** — still triggers at Deity
- **Belief priority order** — still Jesuit Education > alternatives (even if taken)

The core of Arabia's strategy is intact at all difficulties. The adjustments are timing, not direction.
