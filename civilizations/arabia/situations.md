## 1. Barbarians Attacking Early

**What's happening:** Barbarian scouts found your city; camp is spawning units attacking your settlers/workers.

**Why it matters:** Unchecked Barbarians slow expansion, destroy Builder improvements, and can capture settlers.

**What to do:**
- Build a second Warrior immediately
- Use your Scout to kite (don't let Barbarian Scouts return to camp — chase them)
- Clear the camp ASAP: camps drop Gold and sometimes a free Builder
- Position Warriors between the camp and your city/settler

**What not to do:**
- Don't pause Holy Site construction for more than 3–5 turns to handle Barbarians
- Don't let a Scout escape back to camp — it will spawn a wave of attackers

## 2. Another Civ is Rushing Religion

**What's happening:** A nearby civ (Spain, Russia, Ethiopia) is generating Faith fast and will claim early Prophets.

**Why it matters:** Earlier Prophets = better Belief choices. If they claim 5 Prophets before you, your Beliefs may be leftover scraps.

**What to do:**
- Don't panic — Arabia's "Last Prophet" guarantee means you WILL get a religion
- Focus on *which* beliefs are still available, not *if* you'll get a religion
- Prioritise claiming Pantheon (only 25 Faith required) before the rival does
- If your most wanted Beliefs (Jesuit Education, Tithe) are taken, re-evaluate:
  - Jesuit Education taken → use Religious Community + plan to produce Madrasas via Production
  - Tithe taken → use Church Property (Gold per city following)

**What not to do:**
- Don't rush Faith so hard that you delay Holy Site, Shrine, and early expansion
- Don't concede your religion entirely — adjust beliefs and continue

## 3. You Missed the Holy Site Window

**What's happening:** You're at T40–50 and your capital still doesn't have a Holy Site. Others are ahead.

**Why it matters:** Every turn without a Holy Site is lost Faith. The compounding effect of early Faith is significant.

**What to do:**
- Queue Holy Site immediately — drop whatever else is in the queue
- Check if city 2 (or 3) has better Holy Site placement and build one there first
- Pantheon should already be picked — if not, focus purely on reaching 25 Faith
- Accept that you'll get a later Prophet (with Arabia's guarantee, that's okay)
- Pivot: if Holy Sites will be weak (poor adjacency), consider leaning harder on Science Victory with strong Campuses instead

**What not to do:**
- Don't continue building non-Faith infrastructure without addressing the Holy Site gap
- Don't give up on religion entirely — even a modest Holy Site compounds over 200 turns

## 4. Neighbor Declares War Early

**What's happening:** An AI civ has declared war on you in the Ancient or Classical era.

**Why it matters:** Early wars can raze your cities, capture settlers, and delay your entire build plan.

**What to do:**
- Assess their army: if it's 2 Warriors, you can hold with 2 Warriors + city ranged attack
- Build walls if you haven't (Ancient Walls from Masonry tech protect cities)
- Send a delegation/peace offer after holding the first attack — many AIs accept
- Use defensive terrain (hills, rivers) — your units defend with a bonus
- If you can hold the attack, counter-offer peace with no concessions — they may accept

**What not to do:**
- Don't sacrifice your Holy Site build for mass military production unless the threat is existential
- Don't abandon your expansion plan — just delay it by 10–15 turns while holding
- Don't chase the enemy across the map — defend your cities, then make peace

## 5. No Horses on the Map

**What's happening:** You've researched Animal Husbandry and Horses are not accessible in your territory or reachable tiles.

**Why it matters:** Mamluks require Horses. Without them, you lose Arabia's best military unit.

**What to do:**
- Check if Horses appear after claiming tiles via culture borders (Horses appear only after being researched)
- Consider a military action to claim a Horse-rich tile
- Build a trade route to a civ that has Horses — you can receive Horses as a trade resource
- Pivot military to Crossbowmen (ranged) + Pikemen (anti-cavalry) for defense
- For offense without Mamluks: Catapults + Knights (if you can trade for Horses) or Crossbowmen + Musketmen

**What not to do:**
- Don't skip Stirrups entirely — it's still on the tech path to other improvements
- Don't pursue Domination Victory without Horses — Mamluks are the core of that strategy

## 6. Rival Spreading Religion into Your Cities

**What's happening:** You see the icon of a foreign religion appearing in your cities. Their Missionaries are active in your territory.

**Why it matters:** A city following a foreign religion reduces your Tithe income, reduces your religious score, and signals a civ hostile to your victory condition.

**What to do:**
- Identify the civ spreading and assess their intent (are they going for Religious Victory?)
- Position one of your Apostles in the threatened city immediately
- Engage incoming Missionaries with your Apostle in Theological Combat (costs 1 Apostle charge)
- Build/faith-purchase a Missionary to re-spread your religion into the contested city
- If they persist: declare a **Holy War** (formal war type that costs no Diplomatic relationship loss)

**Priority based on severity:**
- 1–2 rival cities spreading → handle with Apostle defense
- 3+ cities spreading → active Missionary countercampaign + consider Holy War
- Your core cities being converted → immediate Holy War; this is existential

**What not to do:**
- Don't ignore spreading — 1 city lost seems minor but it's exponential
- Don't use Inquisitors in distant cities (they're defensive only — use them in your core, not front lines)

## 7. You're Running Low on Faith

**What's happening:** Faith income isn't keeping up with Missionary/Apostle costs. You're spending Faith faster than you earn it.

**Why it matters:** Faith is the currency of Arabia's entire strategy. Running dry stalls religious spreading, Madrasa faith-purchases, and military faith-purchases.

**What to do:**
- Check your Holy Site districts: are they all built? Are Shrines and Temples in each?
- Check Worship Buildings — are they deployed? (Near-free for Vizier; check if they're actually built)
- Check Policy Cards: is Triangular Trade active? Scripture?
- Trade Route audit: are routes going to faith city-states or to your own cities for Production?
- Consider building another Holy Site city if you have an expansion target with good adjacency

**Quick fixes:**
- Switch a Trade Route to a city-state that gives +4 Faith/route (e.g. Nazca, Kandy)
- If in Theocracy: the +5 Gold per Holy Site policy compounds with Trade
- Faith-purchase a Shrine in any city that doesn't have one yet

**What not to do:**
- Don't faith-purchase Missionaries if you can't sustain the Faith drain
- Don't ignore the underlying cause — a short-term Gold buyout doesn't fix low Faith generation

## 8. City Won't Grow — Housing or Amenity Problem

**What's happening:** A city's growth bar is stalled. The tooltip says "Insufficient Housing" or "Low Amenities."

**Why it matters:** Population = more tiles worked = more yields = more everything.

**What to do:**

**Housing fix:**
- Check if city is on a river (if not, build an Aqueduct)
- Build a Granary (Ancient Walls gives 1 Housing as well)
- Build a Neighborhood district (Industrial era)
- Check if Farms are adjacent (gives +0.5 Housing each)

**Amenities fix:**
- Build an Entertainment Complex + Arena (requires Arenas civic)
- Build a Luxury improvement near the city (Plantation, Quarry, etc. — resources give +1 Amenity)
- Trade for Luxury resources from other civs (each unique Luxury gives +1 Amenity)
- If in a Theocracy: your religion's Worship Building may provide +1 Amenity (Stupa)

**What not to do:**
- Don't ignore it — a stalled city at pop 6 when it should be at pop 10 is a massive opportunity cost
- Don't build Entertainment Complex in every city — prioritise the cities with highest yields and most growth potential

## 9. You're Far Behind in Science

**What's happening:** It's T130+ and rivals are researching Industrial-era techs while you're still in Medieval.

**Why it matters:** Being 2+ eras behind in tech means weaker units, no Research Labs, and increasingly impossible Science Victory.

**What to do:**
- Identify the cause: no Madrasas? No Campuses? Wrong beliefs?
- If no Jesuit Education: produce Campuses via Production queue urgently
- Faith-purchase every Madrasa you can (if Jesuit Education is active)
- Check policy cards — is Rationalism equipped?
- Check Trade Routes — international routes can provide Science
- Build Libraries immediately in every Campus that doesn't have one

**Emergency catch-up:**
- Use a Great Scientist if you have one — they can provide large one-time Science boosts
- Consider ally with a Science city-state for bonus Great Scientist points
- Espionage: steal one technology from a leading civ (Spies unlock at Castles)

**Honest assessment:**
If you're 3+ eras behind at T160, Science Victory is probably not achievable this game. Consider pivoting to Religious Victory (if your Faith engine is working) or Domination.

## 10. City-State You Need is Being Taken by a Rival

**What's happening:** Another civ is sending Envoys to a city-state you're suzerain of (or want to be suzerain of).

**Why it matters:** Suzerainty provides powerful bonuses; losing it to a rival gives them those bonuses instead.

**What to do:**
- Prioritise sending more Envoys — you need 3 more Envoys than the rival to reclaim suzerainty
- Check if your Civics research provides bonus Envoys (some do)
- If it's a critical city-state (faith/science): this is worth spending Diplomatic Favor to prevent losing it
- Build an Encampment or Commercial Hub in a city if the city-state is Economic or Military (they give Envoys sometimes)

**Priority city-states for Arabia:**
- **Kandy** (Faith): +2 Amenities from Holy Sites — strong
- **La Venta** (Faith): Additional Pantheon belief — very powerful early
- **Bologna** (Science): +1 Great Scientist slot in each Campus — very strong mid-game
- **Geneva** (Science): Prevents you from going to war (tricky); +6 Science if suzerain

## 11. Another Civ is Winning by Score (Late Game)

**What's happening:** The score timer is running and someone is ahead on score with no active victory path being pursued.

**Why it matters:** If no one wins a standard victory, the highest score at turn limit wins.

**What to do:**
- Check their score sources: population? Wonders? Districts?
- Your fastest score boosts: settle new cities, build more districts, grow population
- Convert to a more achievable victory path before time runs out
- If it's T300+ on Standard, focus on the victory path you're closest to completing

## 12. You Captured a City — Now What?

**What's happening:** You've just captured an enemy city. It's damaged, possibly has foreign religion, and may have negative Loyalty.

**First 5 turns in a new city:**
1. **Don't raze it** (unless it's completely surrounded by enemy territory with no path forward)
2. Assign a **Governor** immediately — reduces Loyalty loss significantly
3. Queue **Shrine** → build it fast → Holy Site eventually
4. Garrison a military unit for +1 Loyalty
5. Spread your religion with an Apostle or Missionary ASAP

**Loyalty management:**
- A captured city with negative Loyalty will eventually revolt and become a free city (or rejoin the original civ)
- If Loyalty is very negative (–10 or worse per turn): assign your highest-influence Governor, put a unit in it, and spread religion quickly
- Theocracy: your religion spreading into the city helps Loyalty

**What not to do:**
- Don't immediately build expensive districts in a captured city — stabilise it first
- Don't ignore the Loyalty bar — losing a city you just fought for is demoralising and wasteful

## 13. A War Is Costing You Too Much

**What's happening:** A war is draining Gold, Faith, and Production without achieving its goals.

**Why it matters:** A costly war delays your entire build plan by 20–40 turns.

**What to do:**
- Assess: have you taken any cities? Is there a path to the goal?
- If Yes (took cities): make peace now — keep what you have
- If No (no progress): make peace immediately — cut your losses
- Peace deal: offer the rival 5–10 turns of Gold per turn — they usually accept if the war stalls
- After peace: consolidate, rebuild army, re-evaluate if the goal is still worth pursuing

**What not to do:**
- Don't escalate a losing war with more unit production — sunk cost
- Don't continue wars past 20–30 turns if no cities have fallen
- Don't make peace on bad terms (giving them cities) unless it's truly hopeless

## 14. You Have a Great Start But Don't Know What to Prioritise

**What's happening:** You've settled well (Mountains, river, Desert Folklore potential) but feel overwhelmed with options.

**What to do:** Follow this priority order:

```
1. Pantheon (Desert Folklore or equivalent) — claim at ~25 Faith
2. Scout the map — find Natural Wonders for Astrology boost
3. Holy Site in capital — highest adjacency location
4. City 2 settled by T25
5. Shrine in capital → Temple in capital
6. Religion founded (T60–80 target)
7. Worship Building in capital (immediately after founding — takes 2 turns)
8. Spread Holy Sites to cities 2 and 3
9. Theocracy government + policy cards
10. Mamluks + Trade Routes + Madrasas simultaneously from T100
```

**Golden start bonus actions:**
- Natural Wonder within 3 tiles → settle adjacent if possible (Tourism + Adjacency)
- Oasis within city radius → high early Food; settle it without delay
- Mountains clustered → plan Holy Site placement carefully for maximum +Faith

## 15. Nearing Religious Victory But Stuck at 45–48%

**What's happening:** You're close to Religious Victory but one or two civs are resisting conversion.

**Why it matters:** Every turn at 48% is a turn your rivals have to catch up or prevent your victory.

**What to do:**
- Identify which cities are stubbornly resisting
- Stack 3 Missionaries + 1 Proselytizer Apostle on the hardest city simultaneously
- Use Proselytizer first (removes all other religions) then Missionaries for your religion
- Check if Open Borders with that civ is active — if not, negotiate or declare Holy War
- Holy War: if a rival is actively countering your religion, Holy War lets you fight theologically AND militarily without diplomatic cost

**Diplomatic acceleration:**
- World Congress: vote for religious-beneficial resolutions
- Offer Luxury trades to civs who are "almost" converted (improves relations → easier Open Borders)
- Religious civs following your religion will vote with you in World Congress — use that leverage

**The final push:**
Calculate exactly how many cities you need. If you need 3 more cities and have 5 Apostles, target 3 specific cities and converge all resources on them simultaneously rather than spreading thin.
