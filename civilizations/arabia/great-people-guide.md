# Great People — Arabia Targeting Guide

*Which Great People to compete for, when, and why.*

*[Policy Cards →](policy-cards-reference.md) | [Tech & Civics →](tech-civics-priorities.md)*

---

## How Great People Work

- Each type (Prophet, Scientist, Merchant, etc.) has a pool of historically named individuals
- They appear one at a time as Great Person Points accumulate
- **First available goes to whichever civ has the most of that type of point**
- Points come from: district buildings, policy cards, Wonders, city-states
- Once taken, that individual is gone — the next one in the pool appears

**How to attract them:** Build relevant districts + buildings, equip relevant policy cards, reach suzerainty of relevant city-states.

## Great Prophets

Arabia's most important Great Person type in the early game.

### How They Work
- There are only **7 Great Prophets** per game
- Each costs progressively more Faith to summon as more are claimed
- First civ to accumulate enough Faith summons them
- Arabia's "Last Prophet" guarantee: if Arabia hasn't founded a religion when the 7th is claimed, they receive it automatically

### Arabia's Target: Prophet #3–5

Getting Prophet #3–5 means:
- Popular Beliefs (Jesuit Education, Tithe) are likely still available
- You get your religion before the spreading game begins in earnest

**How to achieve it:**
- Holy Site with Shrine (done) → +2 Faith/turn
- Desert Folklore (+1 Faith per Desert tile) → +4–8 Faith/turn from Pantheon
- Temple when built → +4 Faith/turn
- Target: 8–15 Faith/turn by Turn 50; this places you mid-pack for Prophet claiming

### If You Get a Late Prophet (6th or 7th)
The game guarantees it happens, but Beliefs may be depleted. Prioritise in this order for remaining choices:
1. Any remaining strong Follower Belief (Feed the World, Religious Community)
2. Tithe if available; Church Property otherwise
3. Accept you'll lean more on Madrasa Science and less on Worship Building compounding

## Great Scientists

Arabia's most important Great Person for the Science Victory path.

### How to Generate Great Scientist Points
- Campus district: +1 point/turn
- Library: +1 point/turn (stacks with Campus)
- University/Madrasa: +1 point/turn
- Research Lab: +2 points/turn
- Policy card — Nobel Prize: +6 points/turn (equip in Wildcard slot from Industrial era)
- City-state — Bologna suzerainty: +1 point per Campus

### Priority Great Scientists by Era

#### Ancient / Classical Era
| Name | Bonus | Pursue? |
|------|-------|---------|
| **Euclid** | Campus tiles provide +1 Science/turn each | `[H]` Strong passive boost |
| **Hypatia** | +1 Science on all Library tiles; free Library in one city | `[H]` Early Science compounding |
| **Zhang Heng** | Boosts Compass and Printing; free tech | `[S]` Good if you need navigation techs |

#### Medieval Era
| Name | Bonus | Pursue? |
|------|-------|---------|
| **Ibn Battuta** | +3 Science from Trade Routes | `[H]` Excellent for Arabia's trade-heavy play |
| **Hildegard of Bingen** | Campus tiles get +1 Faith; +1 Faith from Shrines | `[H]` Dual Science+Faith — perfect for Arabia |
| **Isaac Newton** | Unlocks: Observatories gain +5 Science; boosts Scientific Theory and Physics | `[E]` Major Science boost; rush these techs |

#### Renaissance / Industrial Era
| Name | Bonus | Pursue? |
|------|-------|---------|
| **Galileo Galilei** | +1 Science per adjacent Mountain to all Campuses | `[E]` Arabia has Mountains — this scales massively |
| **Charles Darwin** | +2 Science from every Natural Wonder | `[S]` Situational — depends on map |
| **Janaki Ammal** | +1 Science per Plantation tile worked | `[O]` Only if many Plantations |
| **Dmitri Mendeleev** | Boosts Plastics and Steel; massive bonus to these techs | `[H]` Speeds Industrial-era research |

#### Modern / Atomic Era
| Name | Bonus | Pursue? |
|------|-------|---------|
| **Albert Einstein** | +250 Science immediately; boosts Nuclear Fission | `[E]` Critical for Science Victory timing |
| **Alan Turing** | Boosts Computers; +5 Science per turn per Campus | `[E]` Transformative for late-game Science |
| **Erwin Schrödinger** | Boosts multiple Atomic-era techs; +15 Science per turn | `[E]` Essential for Space Race pace |

### Great Scientist Competition Strategy
1. Equip **Nobel Prize** policy card (+6 points/turn) from Industrial era onward
2. Reach suzerainty of **Bologna** city-state (+1 point per Campus)
3. Build **Research Labs** as soon as Electricity is researched (+2 points per city)
4. Stack Campuses early — 4 Campuses = +4 base points/turn before buildings

> **Target:** 15+ Great Scientist points/turn by Turn 150 (4+ Campuses + buildings + Nobel Prize). This makes you highly competitive for Galileo, Einstein, and Turing.

## Great Merchants

Useful for Arabia's trade-heavy economy but not a primary focus.

### Priority Great Merchants

| Name | Bonus | Pursue? |
|------|-------|---------|
| **Marco Polo** | Grants a free Trade Route permanently | `[H]` Free Trade Route = permanent income; take when available |
| **John Rockefeller** | +1 Resource from Oil; +2 Gold per City | `[S]` Good if Oil is available |
| **Zhang Qian** | +1 Trade Route slot for every city-state you're Suzerain of | `[S]` Excellent if Suzerain of many CS |
| **Colaeus** | +50 Gold; boosts Currency tech | `[O]` Useful if ahead of the Great Merchant curve |

## Great Generals

Arabia uses these differently depending on persona.

| Name | Bonus | Vizier Use | Sultan Use |
|------|-------|-----------|-----------|
| **Sun Tzu** | Write "The Art of War" (+4 Combat Strength to units) | `[S]` | `[H]` |
| **Trưng Trắc** | +5 CS to cavalry units | `[O]` | `[E]` for Mamluks |
| **El Cid** | City Loyalty pressure; +3 CS | `[S]` | `[H]` |

**How to generate Great General points:**
- Encampment district
- Military units winning combat
- Policy card — Military Research (if available)

---

## Great Admirals

Only relevant on water-heavy maps.

- Arabia prioritises land — skip investing in Great Admiral points unless you're on Archipelago

---

## Summary: Arabia's Great Person Priority

| Priority | Great Person Type | Why |
|---------|-----------------|-----|
| **1** | Great Prophet | Guarantees religion + belief selection |
| **2** | Great Scientist | Drives Science Victory; Galileo and Einstein are transformative |
| **3** | Great Merchant (Marco Polo) | Free Trade Route = permanent income |
| **4** | Great General | Mamluk combat bonuses; Sultan > Vizier for these |
| **5** | Great Admiral | Low priority unless island map |

## Quick Checklist: Great Person Infrastructure

By Turn 100, verify:
- [ ] At least 3 Campuses with Libraries (= +6+ Great Scientist points/turn)
- [ ] Nobel Prize policy card equipped (Industrial era: +6/turn)
- [ ] Bologna suzerainty if available
- [ ] Natural Philosophy doubles Campus adjacency → more Scientist points from Madrasas indirectly
- [ ] Great Merchant opportunities: check for Marco Polo in the pool
