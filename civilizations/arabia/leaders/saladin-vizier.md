## Leader Ability: "Righteousness of the Faith"

> Arabia's Worship Building costs 90% less Production to build. The Worship Building grants the city **+10% Science, +10% Faith, and +10% Culture**. Other civilisations following Arabia's religion also get the Worship Building at 1/10th cost.

**Breaking this down:**

### 1. Near-Free Worship Buildings
A standard Mosque or Cathedral costs ~160 Production. Arabia's version costs ~16. This means:
- Build a Worship Building in 1–3 turns instead of 10–20
- Deploy them empire-wide immediately after founding your religion
- Rush them into every new city the moment you settle

### 2. City-Wide Yield Multiplier
The Worship Building provides a **+10% boost to all Science, Faith, and Culture** produced in that city. This is a percentage multiplier — the more developed the city, the more the bonus is worth.

A city producing 30 Science, 20 Faith, and 15 Culture per turn gains:
- +3 Science, +2 Faith, +1.5 Culture — from a building that cost almost nothing

The bonus compounds as the city grows: more population, more districts, more buildings → bigger absolute gains from the 10%.

### 3. Spreads to Your Allies
Civs that adopt your religion also get the Worship Building at 1/10th production cost. This accelerates their deployment — and increases your Tithe income passively as more followers use it.

### Why This Makes Religion Optional to Rush
Because the Worship Building is so cheap to deploy **once religion is founded**, you don't need to found religion early to benefit from it quickly. You can spend your early turns on infrastructure and found religion in the mid-game — then immediately blanket your whole empire with Worship Buildings within a few turns.

## Playstyle Profile

**The Vizier is an Infrastructure Compounder** — build everything first, then let the free religion multiply it.

| Phase | Primary Focus | Religion's Role |
|-------|--------------|----------------|
| Ancient | Settlers, Campuses, Monuments, military | Pantheon only — claim Desert Folklore and move on |
| Classical | More Settlers, Campuses, Holy Site when Astrology Eureka fires | Holy Site planned but not rushed |
| Medieval | Mamluks — build 4–6 and conquer | Religion founded; Worship Buildings deployed in 1–3 turns per city |
| Renaissance | Natural Philosophy → Madrasa Faith peak | Spreading actively; Apostle corps building |
| Industrial | Assess and commit to Science or Religious | Supporting victory push |
| Modern+ | Execute chosen victory | Religion either wins directly or has boosted Science throughout |

**You are most vulnerable:** Early game if you overinvest in Holy Sites and fall behind on Settlers and Campuses.
**You are strongest:** T80–T130 when Mamluks, Worship Buildings, Madrasas, and Trade Routes all come online simultaneously.

## Victory Paths

All three major victories are genuinely viable from the same opening. Choose at T100–120 based on game state.

### Science Victory

**Target:** Complete the Space Race.

**How Arabia gets there:**
1. Infrastructure-first opening → strong Campus network built early
2. Madrasa (Jesuit Education) faith-purchased in core cities — Science scales faster than normal
3. Natural Philosophy doubles Madrasa Faith output, funding more Campuses
4. Democracy government + Rationalism + Nobel Prize policy cards
5. Great Scientists from Bologna city-state and Great Scientist points

**Choose Science when:**
- You have 3+ high-adjacency Campuses before Medieval
- Two or more rivals are spreading aggressive religions
- You're on a large or Archipelago map (Religious Victory is harder)

### Religious Victory

**Target:** Convert 51%+ of all rival cities.

**How Arabia gets there:**
1. Religion founded automatically (Last Prophet) — no early investment needed
2. Worship Buildings deployed empire-wide immediately (near-free, 1–3 turns each)
3. Tithe + Trade Routes → Gold to fund Missionaries and Apostles
4. Missionaries for broad coverage; Apostles (Proselytizer) for contested cities
5. Hagia Sophia wonder: +1 Missionary spread charge + free Apostle promotion

**Choose Religious when:**
- Strong Tithe Gold income scaling from many followers
- Few rival religions remain competitive by Renaissance
- Smaller map size (fewer cities to convert)

### Domination Victory (Secondary)

Mamluks enable a domination path even from the Vizier playstyle. Take the cities, then use Worship Buildings + religion to manage Loyalty in conquered territories.

## Essential Beliefs for Vizier

Because you may be last to claim the Prophet, **check what remains before deciding**. Ideal picks:

### Follower Beliefs
| Belief | Priority | Why |
|--------|---------|-----|
| **Jesuit Education** | `[E]` | Faith-purchase Campus/Theater buildings — including Madrasa |
| **Feed the World** | `[H]` | +3 Food/+1 Housing from Shrines/Temples; strong fallback if Jesuit taken |
| **Religious Community** | `[S]` | +1 Production per 2 followers; good in wide empires |

### Founder Beliefs
| Belief | Priority | Why |
|--------|---------|-----|
| **Tithe** | `[E]` | +1 Gold per 4 followers — scales with every follower across all civs |
| **Church Property** | `[H]` | +2 Gold per following city; frontloaded, falls off vs Tithe at scale |

### Worship Building Beliefs
| Belief | Priority | Why |
|--------|---------|-----|
| **Mosque** | `[H]` | +1 Loyalty/turn to nearby cities — helps hold border cities |
| **Stupa** | `[S]` | +1 Amenity — useful for wide empires needing happiness |
| **Cathedral** | `[S]` | +3 Faith — good if Madrasa Faith is already high |

> **If both Jesuit Education and Tithe are taken:** Feed the World + Church Property still produces a functional religion. Don't panic — the strategy still works.

## Government & Policy Card Strategy

### Government Progression
```
Chiefdom → Oligarchy (early military) → Theocracy [E] → Democracy (late Science)
```

### Theocracy (Medieval Tier) — Core Government
Theocracy: 15% discount on all Faith purchases + +5 Combat Strength in Theological Combat.
- **Simultaneity policy**: Faith-purchase Mamluks for rapid military scaling
- Essential for funding the Apostle corps in the Religious Victory push

## Agenda: "Ayyubid Dynasty"

Saladin's in-game agenda: he values civs that **share his religion** and grows hostile toward those following a different religion or waging war on his co-religionists.

**Diplomacy implications:**
- Converting an AI to your religion improves relations passively and significantly
- Maintain Open Borders with religious allies — spreads religion faster and keeps Saladin friendly
- Don't allow rival religions to dominate your territory if you want to keep AI Saladin as an ally

## Build Order

### Ancient Era
```
Warrior / Slinger / Scout → Monument → Settler → [Warrior] → [Settler]
```
First build is context-dependent. Warrior is the safe default: it explores at 2 movement AND fights Barbarians. Slinger gives the Archery Eureka from its first kill. Scout is best on open large maps with no early threat where the extra movement compounds. Skip Shrine in the capital until your city count and production base are established. The Holy Site will wait for the Astrology Eureka.

### Classical Era
```
Campuses in settled cities → Holy Site (when Astrology Eureka fires) → Shrine → Temple
```
The Astrology Eureka (from discovering a Natural Wonder) halves the tech cost. If you find a Natural Wonder early, build Astrology and then your Holy Site. If not, build other infrastructure first and wait.

### Medieval Era
```
Worship Building (1–3 turns immediately after founding religion) → Mamluk production → Madrasa (faith-buy via Jesuit Education)
```

### General Rules
- Worship Building goes in the same turn (or first turns) after founding religion — it costs almost nothing
- Build Holy Site when the Astrology Eureka fires, not on a fixed turn
- Madrasa should be faith-purchased (Jesuit Education) rather than built when possible
- Keep 4–6 Mamluks active from T85 onward

## City Development Priority

```
Core cities (Capitol + 2–3 best cities):
  Campus → Library → Madrasa (faith-buy)
  Holy Site → Shrine → Temple → Worship Building
  Commercial Hub → Market

Secondary cities:
  Holy Site → Shrine → Temple → Worship Building
  + Campus if good adjacency

Satellite cities:
  Holy Site → Shrine → Worship Building (cheap and fast)
```

### City Tiers for Vizier

Prioritise Campus investment based on Mountain access — this drives Madrasa Faith output:

| Tier | Campus Adjacency | Investment Level |
|------|-----------------|----------------|
| Core | +3 or higher | Full build — Campus, Library, Madrasa, Trade route |
| Secondary | +2 | Holy Site + Campus + Worship Building |
| Satellite | +1 or less | Holy Site + Worship Building only |

## Diplomatic Posture

### Early Game (T1–T80)
- Meet city-states quickly; suzerainty over Science (Bologna) and Faith (Kandy) city-states is high value
- Avoid early wars — you need time to build Settlers and Campuses
- International Trade Routes for Gold income from the moment you have a Commercial Hub or Harbor

### Mid Game (T80–T150)
- Spread religion actively to neighbours via Trade Routes and Missionaries
- Mamluks give you leverage — neighbours will be cautious
- Consider a targeted war to acquire cities with Mountain-rich Campus sites

### Late Game (T150+)
- Maintain Open Borders with civs following your religion (spreads faster)
- Use Apostles aggressively against rival religions
- Protect trade route partnerships for ongoing Gold income

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Rushing Holy Site before T30 | Sacrifices early Settlers and Campuses that compound more | Wait for Astrology Eureka; build infrastructure first |
| Treating religion as primary victory path | Religious, Science, and Domination are all viable — committing too early misses opportunities | Assess at T100; don't lock in before then |
| Skipping Worship Building after founding religion | Missing nearly-free +10% Science/Faith/Culture in every city | Deploy it empire-wide in the same turns you found religion |
| Not using Jesuit Education | Forces slow Campus production instead of faith-purchasing | Prioritise this belief |
| Low-adjacency Campus placement | Limits Madrasa Faith output — the core Faith engine | Plan Campus placement around Mountains before settling |
| Ignoring Mamluks | Arabia's mid-game power spike is one of the best in the game | Build 4–6 and use them at T80–130 |
| Over-investing in Apostle corps before T90 | Apostles are expensive; faith is better spent on Worship Buildings early | Spread via Trade Routes first; Apostles come after Theocracy |

## Comparison: Vizier vs. Sultan

| Aspect | Vizier | Sultan |
|--------|--------|--------|
| **Focus** | Infrastructure + flexible victory | Military conquest |
| **Worship Buildings** | 90% cheaper — deploy empire-wide instantly | Standard cost — selective use |
| **Military bonus** | Indirect (Theocracy Faith-buy for Mamluks) | Direct doubled Flanking bonus |
| **Best VC** | Science or Religious (equal) | Domination |
| **Difficulty** | Beginner-friendly — forgiving opening | Requires combat coordination |

> **For beginners:** Start with the Vizier. The infrastructure-first opening is forgiving, and having three viable victory paths means you can adapt to the map rather than forcing a specific plan.
