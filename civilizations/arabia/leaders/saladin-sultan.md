## Leader Ability: "The Victorious"

> All of Saladin Sultan's military **and** religious units receive **doubled Flanking and Support bonuses**.

**Breaking this down:**

### Flanking Bonuses — What They Are
Flanking occurs when you attack an enemy unit that is already adjacent to another of your units. Normally this grants a small Combat Strength bonus. Sultan's ability doubles this bonus for all units.

- **Military units**: Coordinated attacks from two sides deal significantly more damage
- **Religious units**: Apostles and Missionaries in Theological Combat also benefit — doubling their support/flanking bonuses makes theological offensives considerably more powerful

### Support Bonuses
Support units (e.g. units adjacent to a fighting unit providing buffs) also provide doubled bonuses under The Victorious.

### Strategic Implication
Sultan rewards **unit coordination** over mass production. Two Mamluks attacking from opposite sides of a target deal more damage than two attacking from the same direction. This makes tactical positioning a high-value activity — always consider flanking angles before attacking.

### What Sultan Does NOT Have (vs. Vizier)
- No discount on Worship Buildings (standard production cost)
- No city-wide +10% yield bonus from Worship Buildings

## Victory Paths

### Primary: Domination

**Target:** Capture every other civ's **original capital**.

**How Arabia (Sultan) does it:**
1. Scout aggressively — identify weak neighbours
2. Classical-era push with Warriors + Catapults to knock out 1–2 cities
3. Medieval: Mamluks + combat bonus = surgical strikes deep into enemy territory
4. Each captured city: add Holy Site + Shrine to keep Faith flowing
5. Press forward — Sultan doesn't benefit from sitting still

**Why Arabia is good at Domination:**
- Mamluks heal every turn — deep offensive campaigns without supply lines
- Combat bonus in foreign territory makes every attack stronger
- Gold/Faith income from a wide empire funds continuous military

### Secondary: Religious Victory

Even as Sultan, Arabia's guaranteed religion and Holy Site network still function. The Sultan path to Religious Victory:
1. Conquer neighboring civs' capitals → convert those cities with Apostles
2. A military-religious hybrid: use Holy Wars to legitimise conquest AND spread religion
3. Use conquered cities as forward bases for religious spreading

### Why Not Science?
Sultan can win Science but it's less natural — the build pattern (wide military conquest) competes with the focused, deep Campus development Science requires. Possible but suboptimal.

## Optimal Belief Selections for Sultan

Without cheap Worship Buildings, the belief calculus shifts:

### Founder Beliefs
| Belief | Why |
|--------|-----|
| **Tithe** | Still excellent — more cities from conquest = more followers |
| **Papal Primacy** | City-states following your religion = Faith; useful with military suzerainty |

### Follower Beliefs
| Belief | Why |
|--------|-----|
| **Religious Community** | +Production per followers — fuels military machine |
| **Feed the World** | Growth in newly conquered cities |
| **Warrior Monks** | Build Warrior Monk units with Faith — supplements military |

### Worship Building
For Sultan, Worship Building cost is standard, so choose a building with immediate impactful yields rather than passive ones:
- **Wat**: +2 Science (good for campus support)
- **Stupa**: +1 Amenity (helps conquered cities from revolting)
- **Mosque**: +1 Loyalty (reduces revolt risk in foreign-captured cities)

> **Mosque is especially valuable for Sultan** — conquered cities suffer loyalty penalties; the Mosque's +1 Loyalty per city following your religion helps stabilise a wide empire.

## Government & Policy Cards

### Government Progression
```
Oligarchy [E] (early military) → Monarchy (housing + military) → Theocracy → Fascism or Democracy
```

**Oligarchy** is Sultan's first key government — its free Encampment building and combat policy bonuses are immediately useful.

**Monarchy** (Medieval) provides housing for rapidly-growing captured cities and a strong Military policy slot.

**Theocracy** still valuable once your religion is established — enables Faith-purchasing Mamluks.

**Fascism** (Modern) if continuing Domination: +10% Production for all units; strong late-game military.

### Key Policy Cards

| Era | Slot | Policy | Why |
|-----|------|--------|-----|
| Ancient | Military | **Discipline** | +5 CS adjacent to friendly — maximises flank attacks |
| Classical | Military | **Agoge** | +50% Production for Ancient/Classical units |
| Medieval | Military | **Chivalry** | +7 CS to Medieval cavalry = Mamluks |
| Medieval | Wildcard | **Simultaneity** | Faith-purchase Mamluks (Theocracy) |
| Renaissance | Military | **Veterancy** | +30% Production for Encampment/Harbor districts |
| Industrial | Military | **Levée en Masse** | +10 CS when in own territory; also army maintenance |

## Early Game: Sultan's Opening

Sultan plays faster and more aggressively than Vizier.

### Turn 1–20: Aggressive Setup
```
Scout → Warrior → Settler OR Warrior → Scout → Warrior
```

Two Warriors early is recommended for Sultan — barbarian clearing + early war potential.

**Settle aggressively:** Aim for 3–4 cities by T60 (vs. Vizier's T80). Claim land before neighbors.

### Classical Era War (T30–60)
Sultan's first war window:
- 3 Warriors + 1 Catapult can threaten early cities
- Target: A civ with 1–2 cities that has blocked your expansion corridor
- Don't over-extend — take 1 city, consolidate, then expand again

> **Start the war before you have Mamluks.** The Classical era war secures land that Mamluks will then defend and exploit.

### Religion (T50–80)
Sultan still needs a religion — but it can come slightly later than Vizier:
- Holy Sites in your 2–3 best cities
- "Last Prophet" guarantee removes worst-case risk
- Focus beliefs on military support (Warrior Monks, Religious Community)

## Mamluk Warfare (T80–120)

### Sultan's Combat Advantage
With +5 CS in foreign territory and the Mamluk's healing, your Medieval army is extremely powerful. Use this window decisively.

**Priority targets for Mamluk conquest:**
1. Any remaining civs who control land you want (Mountains, rivers, resources)
2. Civs with weak military (you scouted this in the early game)
3. Civs that are spreading competing religions (Holy War)

### Campaign Tempo
```
Build 6+ Mamluks → Declare War → Take 1–2 cities per campaign
→ Make Peace (keep cities) → Rebuild Mamluks → Next campaign
```

Don't try to eliminate civs in one push unless you're clearly ahead. Stretch your campaigns across 20–30 turns with peace periods.

### Garrison and Stability
Conquered cities need:
- **Military unit** garrisoned (+1 Loyalty)
- **Governor assigned** (reduces Loyalty loss)
- **Religion spread** — your religion provides Loyalty in theocracy; build Shrine immediately
- **Amenities** — entertainment buildings if revolt risk is high

## Mid/Late Game Adaptation

### If You've Won the Military Phase (T130+)
- Consolidate: build Districts and Holy Sites in all captured cities
- Switch to Religious Victory if you have enough cities: spread religion empire-wide
- Or modernise military for late-game Domination push

### If You're Behind Militarily (T130+)
- Dig in with walls and ranged units
- Pivot to Religious Victory — your religion still scales with the city count you have
- Use Gold/Faith income to catch up via Faith-purchasing
