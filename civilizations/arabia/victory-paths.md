## Victory Condition Suitability

All three main victories are genuine, viable paths from the same infrastructure-first opening. The choice should be made at T100–120 based on game state, not at Turn 1.

| Victory | Vizier | Sultan | Notes |
|---------|--------|--------|-------|
| **Religious** | ★★★★☆ | ★★★★☆ | Religion arrives for free; Worship Buildings deploy instantly; Gold scales with followers |
| **Science** | ★★★★☆ | ★★★☆☆ | Madrasa converts Campus investment into Faith and Science simultaneously |
| **Domination** | ★★★★☆ | ★★★★★ | Mamluks are one of the best Medieval units in the game; self-healing conquerers |
| **Culture** | ★★☆☆☆ | ★★☆☆☆ | Viable but Arabia has no innate Culture bonuses beyond Worship Building passive |
| **Diplomatic** | ★★★☆☆ | ★★☆☆☆ | Tithe Gold scales; religious allies vote with you |
| **Score** | ★★★☆☆ | ★★★☆☆ | Arabia's wide yields help; fallback only |

## Religious Victory

### Why Arabia Excels
- Guaranteed religion (no arms race loss)
- Cheap Worship Buildings (Vizier) → passive spread + multi-yield
- Strong Faith engine sustains Missionary/Apostle production
- Tithe scales Gold income with follower count

### Core Requirements
1. Religion founded — arrives automatically (Last Prophet); T60–80 is typical
2. Jesuit Education belief (for faith-buy Madrasas) OR Feed the World
3. Tithe founder belief (Gold scales)
4. Theocracy government by T80–90
5. Hagia Sophia wonder if buildable (T80–120)
6. 5+ active Missionaries/Apostles by T120

### Victory Timeline
| Phase | Target |
|-------|--------|
| T60–80 | Religion founded; Worship Buildings deploying |
| T80–120 | Active spreading; 20–30% of rival cities converted |
| T120–160 | Apostle corps; 40–50% coverage |
| T160–200 | Final push; 51% threshold |

### Counters to Religious Victory
Other civs that are dangerous:
- **Spain** (strong religious bonuses; may spread aggressively)
- **Russia** (passive religious spread from Holy Sites)
- **Ethiopia** (strong Faith generation; founding Prophet early)
- **Mali** (can outproduce Faith with Suguba)

**Response:** Identify these civs early. If they're nearby, convert their cities before they spread back into yours. Use Holy War as justification.

### Pivoting Away from Religious Victory
If by T150 you're below 25% coverage and falling behind:
- Switch to Science Victory (if Madrasa network is strong)
- Do not persist in a losing Religious race — you'll waste Faith that could fund Science buildings

## Science Victory

### Why Arabia Can Do It
- Madrasa generates Faith equal to its Campus adjacency — the Science and Faith engines are the same engine
- Jesuit Education lets you Faith-purchase Madrasas, bypassing slow production
- Strong Gold/Trade income funds other Science buildings
- Religion enhances the Science path rather than competing with it

### Core Requirements
1. Campuses with +3 or higher adjacency in 3+ cities (drives Madrasa Faith output)
2. Jesuit Education belief (mandatory — enables Faith-purchasing Madrasas)
3. Campuses + Libraries in every major city by T120
4. Madrasas faith-purchased in all core cities by T140
5. Research Labs built in Industrial era
6. Space Port in your highest-Production city (Industrial Zone required)
7. Democracy government from Industrial era onward

### Science-Specific Beliefs (Adjust from standard Religion build)
| Type | Belief | Why |
|------|--------|-----|
| Follower | **Jesuit Education** | Faith-buy Science buildings — non-negotiable |
| Founder | **Tithe** | Gold income funds everything else |
| Enhancer | **Just War** | Extra Apostle strength; keep religious network healthy while pursuing Science |

### Victory Timeline
| Phase | Target |
|-------|--------|
| T100–120 | Madrasas in 3+ cities; Science >50/turn |
| T120–150 | Research Labs deploying; Science >80/turn |
| T150–180 | Satellites tech; Space Port under construction |
| T170–200 | Space Race projects launching |
| T200–230 | Victory (varies with game speed and competition) |

### Watch For
- Korea (top Science civ) and Babylon may outpace you if you delay Campuses
- Prioritise Great Scientists aggressively from T100 onward
- Don't over-invest in Religion past T130 if Science is your path

## Domination Victory

### Why Arabia Can Do It
- Mamluks are one of the best Medieval units in the game
- Wide Gold income sustains large armies
- Faith-purchasing units (Theocracy) generates Mamluks fast
- Sultan's combat bonus in foreign territory amplifies every attack

### Core Requirements
1. 4+ Mamluks operational by T90
2. Horses accessible and improved
3. Encampments in military cities (XP generation)
4. Oligarchy → Theocracy → Monarchy → Fascism government path
5. Chivalry + Simultaneity policy cards during Mamluk era

### Victory Timeline
| Phase | Target |
|-------|--------|
| T30–60 | Classical-era expansion (Warriors + Catapults) |
| T80–120 | Mamluk era — primary conquest phase |
| T120–160 | Consolidate; build modernised military |
| T160–200 | Final capitals captured |

### Practical Sequence
1. **T1–50**: Scout all neighbours; identify weakest target
2. **T50–80**: Take weakest neighbour (1–2 cities) with Classical units
3. **T80–120**: Mamluks take main rivals — original capitals
4. **T120–160**: Modern units replace Mamluks; continue west or east
5. **T160+**: Final capitals; diplomacy to isolate remaining civs

### Challenge
Domination requires maintaining military through three unit generations (ancient → medieval → modern). Don't fall behind tech — upgrade Mamluks to Cavalry to Mechanised Infantry as techs unlock, or you'll be fighting modern units with medieval ones.

## Culture Victory (Situational)

### Arabia's Limited Advantage
Arabia has no innate Culture bonuses beyond Worship Building passive Culture yields. This is a secondary path at best.

### When It Makes Sense
- You have a cultural city-state as a suzerain (e.g. Mohenjo-daro, Yerevan)
- You control multiple Natural Wonders (Tourism bonuses)
- Several opponents are also weak at Culture

### Key Requirements
- Theater Squares in 3+ cities (deprioritised normally — consider for this path)
- Open Borders with all civs (maximises Tourism conversions)
- National Parks where terrain allows
- Great Works: Writers, Artists, Musicians filling up slots

## Diplomatic Victory (Situational)

### Arabia's Angle
- Tithe founder belief generates enormous Gold income as followers grow
- Gold = Diplomatic Favor (from World Congress voting)
- Religious-following civs are diplomatically warm → vote with you

### Key Requirements
- World Congress active (unlocks with Political Philosophy civic)
- Accumulate Diplomatic Victory Points via World Congress resolutions
- Suzerainty of Diplomatic city-states
- High Gold income for purchasing Diplomatic Favor

### Realistic Assessment
A full Diplomatic Victory requires sustained World Congress engagement across many eras. Arabia can pursue this as a *secondary* path (alongside Religious) or as a fallback if Religious Victory stalls.

## Choosing and Committing

### Decision Point: T100–120
By T100–120, the game state should determine your path:

```
How many rival cities follow your religion?
  35%+    → Push Religious Victory
  25–35%  → Can recover Religious; watch for 2–3 era turns
  Under 25% → Pivot to Science (if Madrasas strong) or Domination (if military ahead)

What is your Science/turn?
  80+     → Science Victory viable; assess competition
  50–80   → Need more Madrasas; consider hybrid approach
  Under 50 → Science Victory unlikely without major changes

Do you control 2+ rivals' original capitals?
  YES     → Domination is achievable; continue
  NO      → Only commit to Domination if military is strong
```

### The Hybrid Trap
Avoid splitting focus between two victories past T150. Signs you're in the hybrid trap:
- Building Theater Squares when you meant to win by Religion
- Still building Missionaries when you need Space Race production
- Researching Culture civics instead of Science techs

Pick one. Commit. Win.
