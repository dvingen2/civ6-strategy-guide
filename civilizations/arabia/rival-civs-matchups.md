## Religious Rivals

These civs compete directly for Great Prophets, Belief choices, and Religious Victory.

---

### Spain (Isabella)

**Threat level:** High — Religious Victory specialist

**What they do:**
- Strong Faith generation from Holy Sites
- Large religious spread across continents
- Powerful religious units; aggressive Theological Combat
- Cathedral worship building with bonuses

**What to watch:**
- Isabella will rush Great Prophets — she may claim 2–3 before you get one
- Her missionaries are relentless; she'll spread into any undefended city
- She may declare Holy Wars to spread militarily

**Arabia's counters:**
- "Last Prophet" guarantee means you'll still get a religion — don't panic about her early rushing
- **Prioritise Belief selection** — if she gets early Prophets, she gets first pick of beliefs; check what remains and adapt
- **Secure your home cities first** — keep 1 Apostle in each city near her border
- **Spread faster** — match her Missionary output turn-for-turn; your cheap Worship Buildings (Vizier) help here
- If she's next to you: consider a Holy War at Medieval to disrupt her Apostle production

**Key observation:** Spain is often the biggest Religious Victory blocker. Treat her as the primary rival from Turn 1.

---

### Russia (Peter)

**Threat level:** Medium-High — passive religious spread powerhouse

**What they do:**
- Holy Sites generate extra tiles (Russian cities expand borders faster)
- Religion spreads passively from cities even without Missionaries
- Lavra district: unique Holy Site, very high Faith output
- Strong Faith generation; often gets early Prophets

**What to watch:**
- Russia's passive spread is silent — you may not notice until your border cities are converted
- Peter prioritises Science AND Religion — he may outpace you on both if ignored
- Russian cities can be far away; his religion can "creep" across the map slowly

**Arabia's counters:**
- Monitor your border cities with Russia for religion changes — check each turn
- **Trade Routes:** International routes spread your religion to his cities passively (use them)
- If his religion enters your core: Inquisitor in your capital; Apostle on each affected border city
- Focus on converting Russia's cities early (closer targets) — once your religion is dominant there, passive spread helps you

---

### Ethiopia (Menelik II)

**Threat level:** Medium — Strong Faith output, strong in highlands

**What they do:**
- Holy Sites on Hills generate bonus Faith
- Religion provides Science and Culture bonuses
- Starts on Hills (your maps may overlap with Arabia's Mountain preference)
- Strong early Faith; often gets top Prophets

**What to watch:**
- Ethiopia generates Faith faster than Arabia in Hill-heavy starts — may claim Prophet 1 or 2
- The Religion-to-Science conversion they have mimics some of Arabia's strategy
- May be a neighbour on Highland maps

**Arabia's counters:**
- Both civs want Mountains/Hills — you may be competing for the same city sites
- Ethiopia is generally non-aggressive; a diplomatic approach (alliance, sharing religions) may be better than conflict
- If they're nearby: settle aggressively to claim Mountain-adjacent sites before they do
- Your Mamluk is stronger than anything Ethiopia fields at Medieval — if you need to fight, T80–120 is the window

---

### Mali (Mansa Musa)

**Threat level:** Medium — faith purchases and economic dominance

**What they do:**
- Enormous Gold income — can faith-purchase anything at will
- Unique Suguba market building gives discounts on faith/gold purchases
- Can outbuy Arabia's own faith-purchase strategy

**What to watch:**
- Mansa Musa can spam Missionaries with Gold effectively bypassing Faith generation
- His economy scales faster than most civs — don't try to out-gold him
- Often wins Science or Religious Victory via pure purchasing power

**Arabia's counters:**
- Don't compete economically — compete on Faith generation and spreading efficiency
- **Your Worship Buildings are faster to deploy** than anything he can buy — get Worship Buildings empire-wide before he catches up
- **Apostles with Proselytizer** counter his Missionary spam — one Proselytizer negates 3 Missionaries worth of work
- Focus on deep spreading into distant civs he can't reach; let him contest nearby cities and go around him

---

### Georgia (Tamar)

**Threat level:** Low-Medium — City-state faith bonuses

**What they do:**
- Golden Age bonuses from religious suzerainty of city-states
- Strong Envoy income; often controls religious city-states you want
- Faith generation from city-states

**What to watch:**
- Georgia competes for Kandy, Nazca, and La Venta suzerainty
- She may lock you out of the best faith city-states early

**Arabia's counters:**
- Rush Envoys to Kandy first — often the most contested city-state between you and Georgia
- Georgia is not a strong military civ; she's rarely a military threat
- If she has Kandy and you can't beat her Envoy count, pivot to Bologna (Science) or Singapore (Trade)

## Military Threats

These civs may declare war before Arabia is ready.

---

### Aztec (Montezuma)

**Threat level:** High (early game) — aggressive expansion

**What they do:**
- Strong early military (Eagle Warriors convert kills to Builders)
- Very aggressive AI; frequently declares early wars
- Culture bombs (district placement expands borders aggressively)

**What to watch:**
- Aztec AI will often declare war in the Classical era before you have Mamluks
- Their Eagle Warriors are strong — don't underestimate them with Warriors only
- They will settle near you aggressively

**Arabia's counters:**
- Build a third Warrior if Aztec is your neighbour (don't be caught with only 1)
- Walls (from Masonry tech) provide a ranged attack from your city — build them before they arrive
- Make peace after their first attack; they often accept
- Once Mamluks arrive (T80–100): they can no longer threaten you militarily

---

### Mongolia (Genghis Khan)

**Threat level:** High (mid-game) — cavalry-focused civ that outpaces Mamluks in some situations

**What they do:**
- Strong cavalry units with unique bonuses
- High mobility; can reach your cities quickly
- Flanking bonuses that reduce your Mamluk advantage

**What to watch:**
- Mongolia is Arabia's direct cavalry rival in the Medieval era
- Their Keshig (ranged cavalry) can pin your Mamluks while other units attack cities
- Genghis AI declares war frequently

**Arabia's counters:**
- Crossbowmen alongside Mamluks — Keshig are cavalry and take extra damage from Crossbowmen
- Use terrain (hills, rivers) to limit their mobility
- Theological advantage: if Mongolia follows your religion, they won't attack (or attacks are diplomatic-penalty free as Holy War if they follow a different religion)
- **Don't fight Mongolia without Mamluks.** If they attack pre-Medieval, turtle behind walls and make peace.

---

### Zulu (Shaka)

**Threat level:** High (mid/late game) — corps and army formation

**What they do:**
- Can form Corps and Armies earlier and cheaper than anyone
- High unit strength in formation
- Very aggressive; expands militarily

**What to watch:**
- Zulu's military is formidable from Medieval onward
- Shaka AI will declare war to expand; he wants your cities

**Arabia's counters:**
- Mamluks + Crossbowmen as a defensive wall
- Defensive terrain is critical — let them come to you through chokepoints
- After the Medieval window, upgrade Mamluks to Cavalry before he gets Riflemen
- Diplomatic lever: offer to spread your religion to Zulu cities; improves relations (Ayyubid Dynasty agenda response if you're playing as Arabia's AI neighbour, or leverage Saladin's like of shared-religion civs)

---

### Scythia (Tomyris)

**Threat level:** Medium — early cavalry rush

**What they do:**
- Strong Ancient-era cavalry (Saka Horse Archer)
- Attacks wounded units; very aggressive early
- Often rushes neighbours before Walls are built

**What to watch:**
- If Scythia is nearby, expect an attack in the Ancient/Classical era
- Their units are fast — they can reach your Settler before it settles

**Arabia's counters:**
- Two Warriors + walls before Turn 40 if Scythia is your neighbour
- Scythia lacks staying power late game — survive to Medieval and Mamluks neutralise the threat
- Keep your Settler close to your capital if Saka Horse Archers are visible

## Science Rivals (If Pursuing Science Victory)

### Korea (Sejong / Seondeok)

**Threat level:** High — Science specialist that outpaces Arabia by default

**What they do:**
- Seowon district generates Science based on isolation from other districts
- Top Great Scientist generation
- Often finishes Space Race 30–50 turns before Arabia

**Arabia's counters:**
- **Don't race Korea to Science** — instead, use Religion to slow them: if your religion is converting their cities, they're spending Faith fighting you instead of building
- Faith-purchase Madrasas aggressively from T100 to close the gap
- Target the same Great Scientists — Natural Philosophy doubles your adjacency and competes with their output
- If Korea is close to Space Race completion: military strike on their Space Port city (drastic but effective)

---

### Babylon (Hammurabi)

**Threat level:** Very High — fastest Science civ in the game

**What they do:**
- Can research technologies for free by earning Great Scientists
- Snowball tech advantage rapidly
- Often finishes Science Victory 50–80 turns before anyone else

**Arabia's counters:**
- Babylon is the hardest Science rival to compete with — you probably won't win a pure Science race
- **Switch to Religious Victory** if Babylon is your neighbour and is pulling ahead
- Alternatively: aggressive early war to deny them their Campuses (risky and requires strong military)
- Their strength is tech speed, not military — a sustained Holy War disrupts their development
