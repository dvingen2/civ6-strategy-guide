## How Policy Cards Work

- You slot cards into your government's available slots (Military, Economic, Diplomatic, Wildcard)
- Slots vary by government — Theocracy has different slots than Democracy
- Cards provide passive bonuses for as long as they're slotted
- **Review cards whenever:** you research a new civic, change governments, or enter a new era
- Cards can be swapped freely between turns (no cost)

## Arabia's Core Card Stack by Era

### Ancient / Classical Era

| Slot | Card | Bonus | Why Arabia Wants It |
|------|------|-------|---------------------|
| Economic | **Urban Planning** | +1 Production all cities | Faster Holy Sites and districts |
| Economic | **Ilkum** | +1 Builder charge (Builder costs 1 less charge per improvement) | Improves tiles faster; more Faith tiles improved sooner |
| Military | **Discipline** | +5 CS when adjacent to friendly unit | Cheap combat boost for Warrior stacks |
| Wildcard | **Revelation** | +50% Faith generation from Pantheon | Doubles Desert Folklore income early |
| Wildcard | **God King** | +1 Faith, Science, Culture, Production, Gold in capital | Powerful single-city early boost |

> **Best early stack (before Theocracy):** Urban Planning + Revelation + Discipline + (Ilkum or God King)

### Medieval Era (Theocracy active)

**Theocracy slot layout:** 1 Military + 2 Economic + 2 Wildcard (verify in-game — varies with DLC)

| Slot | Card | Bonus | Why Arabia Wants It |
|------|------|-------|---------------------|
| Economic | **Triangular Trade** | +4 Gold, +1 Faith per Trade Route | Core income card; scales with every Trade Route |
| Economic | **Serfdom** | +2 Builder charges (Builders get 2 extra actions total) | Faster tile improvements across your empire |
| Military | **Chivalry** | +7 CS to Medieval/Renaissance cavalry | Direct Mamluk combat buff |
| Wildcard | **Scripture** | +100% Faith from Holy Sites (Medieval/Classical) | Doubles Holy Site Faith output — use until Renaissance |
| Wildcard | **Simultaneity** | Faith-purchase units in any city with a district | Allows Faith-buying Mamluks everywhere |
| Wildcard | **Professional Army** | 50% cheaper unit upgrades | Use when upgrading Knights → Mamluks or during rapid military buildup |

> **Best Medieval stack:** Triangular Trade + Serfdom + Chivalry + Scripture + Simultaneity

### Renaissance Era

| Slot | Card | Bonus | Why Arabia Wants It |
|------|------|-------|---------------------|
| Economic | **Triangular Trade** | +4 Gold, +1 Faith per Trade Route | Keep this slotted permanently |
| Economic | **Market Economy** | +1 Trade Route slot | More routes = more Gold + Faith |
| Military | **Chivalry** | +7 CS to Medieval/Renaissance cavalry | Keep until Mamluks upgrade |
| Wildcard | **Natural Philosophy** | Campus adjacency bonuses doubled | **Doubles Madrasa Faith output** — critical for Arabia |
| Wildcard | **Raj** | +10 influence points from city-states | High if suzerain of science/faith city-states |

> **Natural Philosophy is Arabia's best Renaissance wildcard.** A +4 Campus adjacency becomes +8 adjacency under Natural Philosophy — Madrasa produces +8 Faith/turn in that city instead of +4.

## Permanent Cards (Never Remove These Once Equipped)

| Card | Era Available | Why It Stays |
|------|--------------|-------------|
| **Triangular Trade** | Medieval | +4 Gold +1 Faith per route; scales with Trade Route count forever |
| **Rationalism** | Industrial | +1 Science per Campus; more Campuses = more value, no reason to swap |
| **Natural Philosophy** | Classical | Doubles Campus adjacency → Madrasa Faith; dominant wildcard until end game |

## Sultan-Specific Card Changes

Sultan replaces the Vizier's faith-purchase focus with flanking bonuses:

| Swap | Why |
|------|-----|
| Remove: **Simultaneity** | Less relevant without cheap Worship Buildings |
| Add: **Levée en Masse** or **Retinues** | More military combat bonuses |
| Keep: **Chivalry** | Mamluk buff applies to Sultan too |
| Add: **Oligarchic Legacy** (if switching from Oligarchy) | Free Encampment bonus |

---

## Cards to Skip for Arabia

| Card | Reason |
|------|--------|
| **Navigation Acts** | Sea Trade Route bonuses; Arabia is primarily land-based |
| **Charismatic Leader** | Influence Points per turn; only valuable for Diplomatic Victory |
| **Wars of Religion** | Religious unit bonuses via combat specifically — limited use |
| **Wishful Thinking** | Luxury resources amenities — situational; usually better options available |
| **Film Studio** | Tourism/Culture; not Arabia's path |
| **Propaganda** | Tourism; skip unless Culture Victory |

## Policy Audit Habit

Add this to your routine:
1. **On every era transition** — open the Government screen and check for newly unlocked cards
2. **On every government change** — re-evaluate all slots; don't carry over suboptimal cards
3. **At Turn 100** — verify Triangular Trade and Chivalry are slotted
4. **At Turn 150** — verify Natural Philosophy is in a Wildcard slot; verify Rationalism is in Economic
