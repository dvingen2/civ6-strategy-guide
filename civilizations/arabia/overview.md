## What Makes Arabia Different

Most civs must choose between investing in religion or investing in infrastructure. Arabia doesn't. The Last Prophet guarantee means you will always found a religion — so you can spend your early turns building Campuses, Settlers, and military while religion catches up on its own.

Once Mamluks arrive in the Medieval era, Arabia has one of the game's strongest mid-game power spikes: durable self-healing cavalry that can conquer territory, acquire infrastructure, and pressure any neighbour. From that position of strength, you assess the game state and commit to whichever victory path is most open.

Religion throughout all of this functions as a **yield multiplier** — cheap Worship Buildings boosting Science, Faith, and Culture across every city; Tithe Gold flowing from follower count; Madrasas converting Campus investment into Faith. It enhances whatever you're doing rather than dictating the path.

## Civilization Unique Ability: "The Last Prophet"

> If Arabia has not yet founded a religion when the last Great Prophet is claimed, Arabia automatically receives that Great Prophet.

**The correct interpretation:** This ability is a licence to *not* rush Faith. Other civs must invest heavily in Faith early to secure a Prophet and found a religion. Arabia can skip that investment entirely and receive a guaranteed religion later — potentially with decent Beliefs remaining, because AI civs pick them poorly at all difficulty levels.

**In practice:**
- Build Settlers, Campuses, and military instead of Holy Sites in the early game
- Wait for the Astrology Eureka (discovering a Natural Wonder) before building your Holy Site — this halves the tech cost and removes timing pressure
- Your religion will arrive. The question is whether your infrastructure is strong enough to use it effectively when it does.

**Belief quality at late claiming:** Community experience confirms AI civs select Beliefs sub-optimally even at Deity. Jesuit Education and Tithe are frequently still available to a late-arriving Prophet. Build order: check what remains before panicking about being last.

## Unique Unit: Mamluk

| Stat | Mamluk | Knight (Standard) |
|------|--------|------------------|
| **Replaces** | Knight | — |
| **Era** | Medieval | Medieval |
| **Strength** | 48 | 48 |
| **Movement** | 4 | 4 |
| **Resource** | Horses | Horses |
| **Unique Ability** | Heals every turn regardless of actions | — |

**Healing rates:** 20 HP/turn in City Centers, 15 in friendly territory, 10 in neutral, 5 in hostile.

### Why Mamluks Define Arabia's Mid-Game

Standard cavalry can only heal by skipping a turn. Mamluks heal *while fighting* — every turn, automatically. This changes offensive warfare fundamentally:

- **No supply lines needed:** Push deep into enemy territory without stopping to recover
- **No need to rotate damaged units:** Keep the pressure on; the unit heals as it fights
- **Attrition resistance:** The enemy can't simply wait out a siege — your cavalry never degrades
- **Conquest speed:** 4–5 Mamluks can take multiple cities in a single campaign

The Mamluk mid-game window (roughly T80–T130) is Arabia's primary strategic moment regardless of which victory you're pursuing. Use it to acquire cities, territory, and enemy infrastructure — or simply to remove threats and dominate your neighbours.

## Unique Building: Madrasa

| Stat | Madrasa | University (Standard) |
|------|---------|----------------------|
| **Replaces** | University | — |
| **District** | Campus | Campus |
| **Science** | +5 base | +4 base |
| **Faith** | **+Faith = Campus adjacency bonus** | None |
| **Great Scientist slot** | 1 | 1 |

**How the Madrasa generates Faith:** The Madrasa produces Faith equal to its Campus district's adjacency bonus. A Campus adjacent to 4 Mountains has +4 Science adjacency — the Madrasa adds +4 Faith/turn on top of its base +5 Science.

This means **Campus placement is Arabia's most important city-planning decision**. Mountains near your Campus give you Science from adjacency AND Faith from the Madrasa simultaneously. The Holy Site and Campus both benefit from the same Mountain tiles, but the Madrasa's Faith output is tied to the Campus, not the Holy Site.

**Natural Philosophy policy card** (unlocked via Recorded History civic) doubles Campus adjacency bonuses — and by extension doubles the Madrasa's Faith output. A +4 Campus becomes +8 with Natural Philosophy active. This is one of Arabia's strongest mid-game power plays.

## Arabia's Actual Strategic Logic

```
Infrastructure-first opening
(Settlers + Campuses + military, not Holy Sites)
          ↓
Last Prophet fires automatically — religion founded for free
          ↓
Cheap Worship Buildings deployed empire-wide (Vizier)
(+10% Science/Faith/Culture in every city)
          ↓
Mamluk mid-game conquest (T80–T130)
(acquire territory, enemy cities, enemy infrastructure)
          ↓
Natural Philosophy active — Madrasa Faith doubles
          ↓
Assess game state at T100–120:
  Strong Science lead → Science Victory
  Many followers + Worship Buildings → Religious Victory
  Military dominance → Domination
```

Religion arrives in the middle of this sequence — not at the start.

## Strengths

- **Zero Prophet race pressure** — guaranteed religion removes the biggest early-game risk other religious civs face
- **Flexible victory** — all three main paths genuinely viable from the same opening
- **Mamluk dominance** — one of the best Medieval units in the game, self-sustaining
- **Free yield multipliers** — Worship Buildings are cheap (Vizier) and boost everything
- **Faith from Science** — Madrasa converts Campus investment into Faith passively
- **Strong Gold** — Tithe + Trade Routes fund faith-purchases and unit upkeep

## Weaknesses

- **Campus-dependent** — Madrasa requires Campus, Library, and good adjacency; poor starts limit the Faith engine
- **Horses required** — Mamluks need Horses; Horse-scarce maps reduce mid-game impact
- **Delayed religion** — late Beliefs may occasionally be depleted of top choices
- **No unique district** — unlike Korea or Mali, Arabia has no custom district; relies on standard district stacking

## Recommended Pantheons

| Pantheon | Benefit | When to Take |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| **Desert Folklore** | +1 Faith from Desert tiles | `[E]` Default choice with ≥4 Desert tiles — Arabia's start bias makes this reliable |
| **Stone Circles** | +2 Faith from Quarries | `[S]` Multiple Quarry resources nearby |
| **God of the Open Sky** | +1 Culture from Pastures | `[S]` Dense Pasture resources at start |
| **Monument to the Gods** | +15% Production for Ancient/Classical wonders | `[S]` Targeting Stonehenge or Pyramids |
| **Earth Mother** | +1 Faith from Mines on Luxury/Bonus resources | `[S]` Resource-rich start |

Pantheons are claimed at 25 Faith — you'll reach this naturally through Desert Folklore tiles or early Shrine production without a dedicated Faith rush.

## Recommended Belief Selections

Because you may be claiming the last Prophet, **check what remains before deciding**. Ideal picks, in priority order:

### Follower Beliefs
| Belief | Benefit | Notes |
|--------|---------|-------|
| **Jesuit Education** | Campus/Theater buildings purchasable with Faith | `[E]` Enables faith-buying Madrasas — transforms Arabia's Science engine |
| **Feed the World** | Shrines/Temples +3 Food, +1 Housing | `[H]` Strong growth; good fallback if Jesuit taken |
| **Religious Community** | +1 Production per 2 followers (max +8) | `[H]` Amplifies production in widely-followed cities |

### Founder Beliefs
| Belief | Benefit | Notes |
|--------|---------|-------|
| **Tithe** | +1 Gold per 4 followers | `[E]` Scales with every follower across all civs |
| **Church Property** | +2 Gold per following city | `[H]` Frontloaded gold; falls off vs. Tithe at scale |

> **If both Jesuit Education and Tithe are taken:** Feed the World + Church Property still produces a functional, solid religion. Don't panic — the strategy works, it's just slightly less efficient.

## Optimal City Placement

### Capital
- Prioritise: **Campus adjacency** — Mountains within 3 hexes of a Campus placement
- River adjacency for Housing and Commercial Hub
- Desert tiles for Desert Folklore Faith income
- Oasis tiles within city radius are high-value Food sources

### Expansion Cities
- Each core city needs a **Campus with ≥2 adjacency** — this is more important than Holy Site quality
- River access for Housing and trade route connectivity
- Holy Sites are valuable but secondary to Campus planning

### The Mountain Rule
A Mountain tile adjacent to both your Holy Site AND your Campus in the same city contributes:
- +1 Faith to Holy Site adjacency (direct Faith generation)
- +1 Science to Campus adjacency (which Madrasa converts to +1 Faith/turn bonus)

Plan both districts around the same Mountain cluster where possible.
