## Technology Tree

### Ancient Era

| Tech | Priority | Why | Boost Condition |
|------|----------|-----|----------------|
| **Animal Husbandry** | `[H]` | Reveals Horses (needed for Mamluks); pasture yields | Own a Pasture tile |
| **Pottery** | `[E]` | Unlocks Shrine → Faith generation begins | Build a Farm |
| **Mining** | `[H]` | Hills/resource improvement; often needed early | Mine a resource |
| **Irrigation** | `[S]` | If you have Farms on Floodplains/Grassland | Farm adjacent to a River |
| **Writing** | `[E]` | Unlocks Library + Trade Route envoys | Build 2 Districts |
| **Astrology** | `[E]` | **Unlocks Holy Site district** — your most critical early tech | Find a Natural Wonder |

> **Order:** Pottery → Astrology → Writing → Animal Husbandry (or reverse Husbandry/Writing based on start)

**Boost strategy:** Scout aggressively to find a Natural Wonder early — halves the cost of Astrology, your most critical Ancient tech.

### Classical Era

| Tech | Priority | Why | Boost Condition |
|------|----------|-----|----------------|
| **Currency** | `[H]` | Unlocks Market → gold income; opens Commercial Hub | Trade with another civ |
| **Mathematics** | `[H]` | Unlocks Catapult (siege); +1 Trade Route via Policy | Build a Quarry |
| **Horseback Riding** | `[E]` | Unlocks Stable; prerequisite for Heavy Cavalry path | Own a Pasture improvement |
| **Iron Working** | `[S]` | Swordsmen for defense; walls | Build a Barracks or Stable |
| **Construction** | `[S]` | Aqueduct (Housing); Builders can chop forests | Build a Water Mill |
| **Engineering** | `[O]` | Roads, Aqueduct buildings | Chain 3 Pastures |

> **Priority:** Horseback Riding → Currency → then based on situation

### Medieval Era

| Tech | Priority | Why | Boost Condition |
|------|----------|-----|----------------|
| **Education** | `[E]` | **Unlocks Campus + Madrasa** — your Science engine | Build a University in any city |
| **Stirrups** | `[E]` | **Unlocks Knight line → Mamluk** — critical military | Have a Stable and Horses |
| **Military Tactics** | `[H]` | Pike/Shot units for defence; Flanking bonuses | Kill 3 units |
| **Castles** | `[H]` | Unlocks Espionage; Medieval Walls | Conquer or build a Encampment |
| **Machinery** | `[S]` | Crossbowmen (strong ranged) | Kill a unit with a Slinger |

> **Priority:** Stirrups → Education → (Castles if threats exist)

**Boost note:** Education's boost requires a University — meaning you need a Campus first. Build at least one Campus before queuing Education if possible, or accept unbosted research.

### Renaissance Era

| Tech | Priority | Why | Boost Condition |
|------|----------|-----|----------------|
| **Printing** | `[H]` | +1 Trade Route slot; Great Scientist points | Build 2 Universities/Madrasas |
| **Square Rigging** | `[S]` | Frigate (naval); +1 Trade Route slot | Sail on Ocean tile |
| **Astronomy** | `[S]` | Observatory → Campus building +2 Science | Build a University |
| **Metal Casting** | `[O]` | Workshops → Production | Build an Encampment |
| **Gunpowder** | `[H]` | Musketman (key infantry upgrade) | Build a Mine on Strategic resource |

### Industrial Era

|------|----------|-----|----------------|

## Civics Tree

### Ancient Era

| Civic | Priority | Why | Boost |
|-------|----------|-----|-------|
| **Code of Laws** | `[E]` | Unlocks Craftsmanship; first civic policies | Build Ancient Walls or Granary |
| **Craftsmanship** | `[E]` | Builder charges; production policy slots | Improve 3 tiles |
| **Foreign Trade** | `[E]` | Trade Routes; Commercial Hub | Discover a Natural Wonder |
| **Early Empire** | `[E]` | Colonisation (cheap Settlers); expansion | Have a city with 6 pop |
| **State Workforce** | `[H]` | Tiles worked policies; District planning | Build any District |

### Classical Era

| Civic | Priority | Why | Boost |
|-------|----------|-----|-------|
| **Theology** | `[E]` | **Unlocks Theocracy path**; Temple construction | Build a Holy Site |
| **Political Philosophy** | `[E]` | Unlocks governments including Oligarchy | Meet 3 city-states |
| **Drama & Poetry** | `[S]` | Theater Square; cultural policies | Build any specialty district |
| **Games & Recreation** | `[S]` | Amenities; Entertainment Complex | Build 3 different specialty districts |
| **Military Tradition** | `[H]` | Eureka boosts for other civics | Kill a barbarian unit |

### Medieval Era

| Civic | Priority | Why | Boost |
|-------|----------|-----|-------|
| **Feudalism** | `[H]` | +1 Builder charge (key for development); serfdom policy | Build 3 farms |
| **Civil Service** | `[H]` | Merchant Republic option; extra policy slots | Advance era or build districts |
| **Mercenaries** | `[S]` | Extra military policy slots | Own 8 land combat units |
| **Guilds** | `[H]` | Market/Bank buildings available; gold policies | Build a Market |
| **Reformed Church** | `[E]` | **Unlocks Theocracy** — critical government for Vizier | Build a Holy Site in 6 cities |

> **Theocracy is your most important Medieval civic.** Theocracy allows Faith-purchasing military units, applies a 15% discount to all Faith purchases, and gives your religious units +5 Combat Strength in Theological Combat. Rush Reformed Church.

## Vizier Optimal Research Path

### Turns 1–50 (Ancient → Early Classical)
```
TECH PATH:
Pottery → Astrology → Writing → Animal Husbandry → Horseback Riding

CIVIC PATH:
Code of Laws → Craftsmanship → Foreign Trade → Early Empire → Political Philosophy
```

### Turns 50–100 (Classical → Medieval)
```
TECH PATH:
Currency → Stirrups → Education (boost first if possible)

CIVIC PATH:
Theology → Military Tradition → Feudalism → Guilds → Reformed Church [PRIORITY]
```

### Turns 100–150 (Medieval → Renaissance)
```
TECH PATH:
Education complete → Castles (if needed) → Printing → Astronomy

CIVIC PATH:
Reformed Church → [switch to Theocracy] → Humanism → The Enlightenment
```

### Turns 150+ (Renaissance → Industrial → Space)
```
TECH PATH (Science Victory):
Scientific Theory → Electricity → Computers → Satellites → Robotics → Nuclear Fusion

CIVIC PATH:
Democracy → Mobilization → Space Race
```

## Key Boosts Cheat Sheet

Trigger these before queuing the associated research:

| Research | Boost Condition | How to Trigger |
|----------|----------------|----------------|
| Astrology | Find a Natural Wonder | Scout in all directions early |
| Writing | Build 2 Districts | Build Holy Site + another district |
| Currency | Trade with another civ | Send early Trader |
| Education | Build a University | Build a Campus first |
| Reformed Church | Holy Site in 6 cities | Expand steadily; always include Holy Site |
| Humanism | Build 2 Campus Districts | Standard expansion |

## What to Skip (Arabia)

| Tech/Civic | Why Skip |
|-----------|---------|
| Harbor-dependent civics (unless island map) | Arabia plays land-focused |
| **Cultural wonders** like Colosseum, Eiffel Tower | Not your primary yield driver |
| Late-game Religion civics if Science victory | Pivot cleanly when committed |
| Fascism | Production bonus not worth playstyle mismatch |
