## Priority Tier Legend

Used throughout the framework to signal how important an action or choice is:

| Tier | Symbol | Meaning |
|------|--------|---------|
| Essential | `[E]` | Always do this. Non-negotiable for the strategy to work. |
| High Priority | `[H]` | Do this soon. Strong value, few exceptions. |
| Situational | `[S]` | Do this when the conditions are right. Read the context. |
| Optional | `[O]` | Nice bonus if you have the time/production/resources. |
| Skip | `[–]` | Rarely worth it for this civ/leader. Deprioritise. |

## Difficulty Baseline

Unless noted, all guidance assumes **Prince difficulty** (neutral AI, fair starts).

- **Settler/Chieftain/Warlord**: More forgiving. Most strategies work. Focus on learning.
- **Prince**: Baseline. Strategies as written.
- **King/Emperor**: AI gets production/yield bonuses. Prioritise faster scaling.
- **Immortal/Deity**: AI gets large advantages. Tighter openings, less experimentation.

Deity-specific notes are marked `[Deity]` where they differ significantly.
